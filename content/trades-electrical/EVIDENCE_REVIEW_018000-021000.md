# Evidence review: electrical lectures 018000–021000

**Editorial review date:** 2026-11-15. **Status:** technical educational drafts with claim-specific citations and open review actions. No licensed field validation is claimed.

## What was reviewed

- 018000: MIT's named nodal-analysis authors, the arbitrary reference node, conservation laws, return paths, and assumptions of the illustrative resistor network.
- 019000: NIST/NBS multimeter input-characteristic research, NIST measurement uncertainty principles, Fluke's resistance and clamp-meter explanations, and calculated loading and burden examples.
- 020000: measurement category *scope* from IEC 61010-2-030:2023, published IEC 61557-1 scope, UK HSE GS38, OSHA's distinct U.S. workplace scope, and manufacturer explanations of capacitively induced/phantom voltages.
- 021000: NASA's August 2002 aerospace fault-tree handbook (lead author **Dr. William Vesely**, coordinator and coauthor **Dr. Michael Stamatelatos**, plus listed contributors), DOE-STD-1197-2024 causal-analysis methodology, and the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy's account of Bayes' theorem.

## What remains open

1. **Complete standards access:** the published IEC scope descriptions verify relevant standard families, but not all current normative test clauses or local adoption. Do not turn abstracts into installation-specific tests or claims of compliance.
2. **Qualified electrical review:** independent professional review is pending for interpretation of meter CAT ratings, fault isolation/verification, instrument connection failure modes, and use of field diagnostic techniques.
3. **Manufacturer specificity:** the instrument examples show how *particular* product designs behave. Never generalize one impedance, beeper threshold, response time or CAT marking to all instruments.
4. **Original reports and attribution:** NISTIR 88-4021's text was accessible and examined through publisher extraction; its very large PDF could not be visually opened in the web viewer. Its exact pagination and historical instrument implementations merit verification against the primary PDF. The NASA handbook authors and DOE committee are credited in the companion instead of assigning those methods to this course.
5. **Examples remain hypothetical:** circuit values and candidate failure trees are independently reasoned classroom models. They are not instructions for measuring energized building wiring, and do not establish how a real installation is connected.
6. **Source independence:** two vendor pages from the same manufacturer do not count as independent corroboration. Multiple sources in the same university or agency are labeled accordingly. Claims with single-source coverage remain marked as such.

## Editorial choice about narration

Safety distinctions are taught where they explain the physical method. Repeated boilerplate warnings are omitted. The detailed conditions, scope limits, and professional-review tasks are collected here and in structured per-claim evidence metadata. No fixed lecture length, minimum word count or ten-minute target is used.
