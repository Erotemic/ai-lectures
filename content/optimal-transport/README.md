# Optimal Transport: Couplings, Duality, and the Geometry of Probability

Six complete audio-first mathematics lectures connect the finite transportation problem to probability, convex duality, Wasserstein geometry, network algorithms, entropic regularization, and displacement interpolation. The course assumes elementary probability, algebra and calculus and introduces more advanced measure-theoretic statements with their hypotheses.

## Listening order

- `optimal-transport:001000` — **The Mass Has to Go Somewhere**: coupling tables, marginal constraints, Monge versus Kantorovich, one explicit optimum.
- `optimal-transport:002000` — **Prices That Prove a Shipment Optimal**: finite dual potentials, complementary slackness, the one-Lipschitz dual.
- `optimal-transport:003000` — **A Distance Between Distributions**: Wasserstein metrics, the gluing argument, quantile transport and tail behavior.
- `optimal-transport:004000` — **When Transport Becomes Matching**: doubly stochastic matrices, assignment integrality and min-cost flow.
- `optimal-transport:005000` — **Smoothing a Sharp Transport Plan**: entropy, the Gibbs kernel, Sinkhorn row/column scaling and numerical choices.
- `optimal-transport:006000` — **Moving a Distribution Along a Geodesic**: monotone maps, displacement interpolation, Brenier's theorem and dynamic transport.
