# Possible deeper work after the foundational course

The six recorded manuscripts form a complete introduction, not a promise of an indefinitely growing sequence. Develop additional lectures when a mathematical question needs them.

- **Existence and duality beyond finite spaces:** lower semicontinuity, tightness, cyclic monotonicity, measurable selection and proper hypotheses.
- **Statistical optimal transport:** finite-sample estimation, curse of dimensionality, empirical measures and bias.
- **Advanced computation:** unbalanced transport, sparse/partial transport, barycenters, sliced Wasserstein, differentiating regularized solvers, numerical verification.
- **Geometric PDE:** continuity equations, Benamou–Brenier, Monge–Ampère, entropy displacement convexity and curvature-dimension conditions.

Do not conflate balanced OT with unbalanced variants, exact costs with regularized losses, or measurable plans with maps.
