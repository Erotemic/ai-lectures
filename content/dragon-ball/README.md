# Dragon Ball: From Penguin Village to the End of Z

**Status: started — twenty-four complete, source-annotated audio essays.** This is the active, unified track for Akira Toriyama's early work, *Dr. Slump*, the original *Dragon Ball* adventure, and the material adapted as *Dragon Ball Z*. It **supersedes the earlier proposed `dragon-ball-z` plan**. The twenty-four published lectures are real spoken manuscripts, not placeholders; remaining ideas are in [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md), not in the published `track.json`.

## Why begin before Goku?

Toriyama's manga did not spring into existence in 1984, and it did not spring into existence already shaped like *Dragon Ball Z*. A design-trained gag cartoonist spent years finding a voice. His first major success was *Dr. Slump*, built on a robot girl, irresponsible adults, gadgets, wordplay and a village where every encounter could become a joke. From that comic imagination emerged an adventure with a boy, a girl, a radar, and seven magical balls. The martial-arts epic grew gradually out of this earlier world.

The track should be **fun to listen to for fans who know the stories**. Treat iconic scenes as opportunities to analyze character, drawing, page rhythm, animation, voices, adaptations, and creator testimony. Supply enough context to welcome a new listener; do not make an encyclopedia, recount every chapter, or pretend all dubs and television editions are the same text. Essay length follows substance, not a timetable. The aim is the same editorial quality as the existing Spider-Man fan essays.

## Listening routes

- **From the Beginning** (default) — all twenty-four essays in sequence, continuing forward as new manuscripts are completed. The central plot of *Dragon Ball* begins at `003000`, but the series deliberately opens with the artist and *Dr. Slump*.
- **Before Goku** — two substantive, independently listenable essays on Toriyama and *Dr. Slump*.
- **Start with Goku** — `003000` onward for listeners who want to begin with Goku, including Namek, Frieza and Vegeta’s later journey, without *Dr. Slump* prerequisites.
- **Character and Drawing** — Goku, Toriyama’s visual design, Piccolo, Vegeta and the Super Saiyan image (`011000`, `012000`, `018000`, `017000`, `023000`), including some later-story spoilers.
- **Namek, Frieza, and Vegeta** — four finished long-form essays grouped by theme, including the first Super Saiyan, rather than numerical ID. The Vegeta portrait covers later storylines in full.
- **Turning Points and Futures** — five complete essays on Super Saiyan, Trunks, Piccolo, the horror of Cell and Gohan’s victory.
- **The Androids and Cell** — Future Trunks, Cell, Gohan, Bulma and Krillin, and Piccolo; a complete spoiler-rich themed route. Future fan routes should include only finished manuscripts, with the eventual historical capstone last.

## Scope and working conventions

- Main manga: Toriyama's single *Dragon Ball* serial in *Weekly Shonen Jump* (1984–1995). The later *Z* division primarily reflects television and English publishing. Earlier *Dr. Slump* (1980–1984) and selected one-shots receive serious attention in their own right rather than being treated solely as prototypes for Goku.
- Television: original *Dr. Slump* anime beginning 1981; original *Dragon Ball* beginning 1986; original *Dragon Ball Z* beginning 1989; later *Kai* and regional dubs only when the distinction advances analysis. Live-action and later sequels are not required for the main *Z*-era track.
- Films, specials and alternate continuity: discuss as such; never insert them silently into the main manga. Respect different release dates, editions, naming practices and soundtrack histories.
- Spoilers: summaries before spoken text; character and saga essays may discuss endings openly.
- Historical claims: distinguish directly documented publication/production facts, attributed retrospective interviews, and the essay's own critical interpretation. `metadata/sources.json` has the shared bibliography and each `metadata/NNNNNN.json` resolves claim anchors to it. Original source chapter references should accompany any later granular fight analysis.
- Source media: no licensed manga scans, anime stills, clips, music, fonts, or binary artwork in text overlays. Sources and topical links are non-spoken.
- Period/geography: tags include Japan, Aichi, Nagoya, Tokyo, 1970s/1980s and manga history when appropriate. These are history-of-creation tags; do not confuse them with fictional geography.

## Continuing the track

The published manuscripts now include the 1986 anime, Saiyan invasion, Namek, Frieza, Super Saiyan, Future Trunks, Cell’s arrival and the Cell Games, with full portraits of Piccolo and Vegeta and a human-scale essay on Bulma and Krillin. The next substantial story arc is Buu; Goku and Gohan portraits, the drawing and anime-production essays, and the long historical capstone remain to be written. See [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md) for the larger editorial map. The final historical essay should be written after enough of the track exists to support a genuine synthesis.
