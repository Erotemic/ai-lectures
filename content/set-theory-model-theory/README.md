# Set Theory and Model Theory: From Collections to Models

A connected, audio-first foundations course in the mathematics of infinity, axioms, and formal structures. The manuscripts distinguish theorem statements, proof obligations, and the consequences of particular axioms; each has a non-spoken technical companion with exact notation, exercises, solutions, and reading links.

## Other useful routes

- **Sets and infinity:** `001000`–`004000` — axioms, cardinality, ordinals, and choice.
- **Sentences and models:** `005000`–`007000` — first-order satisfaction, completeness, compactness, and elementary substructures.
- **Proofs and limitations:** `005000`–`008000` — what proof systems capture and what effective theories cannot settle.

## Preparation and neighboring tracks

- `mathematical-proof:001000` and `:009000`: proof methods, quantifiers, sets, functions, and induction.
- `logic-types:001000`–`:004000`: propositions-as-types, natural deduction, quantifiers, and inductive constructions, which use a distinct foundational viewpoint.
- `algorithms-computability-complexity:004000`: halting and algorithmic undecidability, helpful for lecture `008000`.
- `abstract-algebra`: independent sets and vector-space bases used as a worked Zorn example.

The structure and existence theorems are classical first-order mathematics, not claims that the library has formalized these proofs in Lean. Theorem qualifications, such as countable language, ambient choice, and effective axiomatizability, are stated when relevant.
