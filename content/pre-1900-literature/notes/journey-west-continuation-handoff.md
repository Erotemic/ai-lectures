## Completed ending, maintenance and review

**The selected-witness chapter-100 ending is now narrated in lecture `573000`.** There is no next prose chapter and no open plot cliffhanger. The completed final stretch:

- `567000`, chapter 94: the disciples appear in the Tianzhu court, Xuanzang remains the intended groom, the garden banquets and wedding preparation proceed, and Wukong slips back into the palace as a bee. **The impostor's nature is not disclosed in this chapter.**
- `568000`, chapter 95: Wukong exposes the false princess, fights the lunar Jade Rabbit, and Taiyin reclaims it. The real princess is freed from confinement at Gold-Spreading Temple; royal patronage and an eventual farewell follow.
- `569000`, chapter 96: Kou Hong completes his twenty-four-year vow of feeding ten thousand monks by receiving the four pilgrims. Xuanzang insists on continuing, despite the family's extended hospitality. Their public farewell attracts the robbers.
- `570000`, chapter 97: bandits kill Kou Hong, his wife and sons falsely accuse the monks, officials arrest and threaten them, and Wukong obtains Kou Hong's soul from Kshitigarbha to revive the householder and refute the accusation.
- `571000`, chapter 98: Jade-True Monastery, the one-log bridge and bottomless boat, shedding the mortal body, reception by the Buddha, blank scriptures, Dipankara's intervention, exchange of the purple-gold bowl and delivery of 5,048 written volumes. The guardians lift the party east at the chapter's end.
- `572000`, chapter 99: Guanyin sees eighty ordeals and requires an eighty-first. Heaven-Reaching River's old turtle throws the party into the water after Xuanzang fails to answer his promised question. Scriptures are wet, pages of the *Sutra of the Buddha's Life* stick to drying stone, and the pilgrims visit the Chen household before departing secretly with the heavenly guardians.
- `573000`, chapter 100: brief return to Chang'an, Emperor Taizong's reception and scripture-copying arrangements, return to Spirit Mountain, distinct rewards for Xuanzang, Wukong, Bajie, Sha and the dragon-horse, and Wukong's discovery that the fillet has disappeared. **This closes the novel.**
