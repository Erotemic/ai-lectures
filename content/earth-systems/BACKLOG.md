# Earth Systems Course Backlog

Forty-five complete, indexed lectures. The established sequences cover Earth-system foundations, ENSO, hazards, biogeochemistry, paleoclimate reconstruction, numerical Earth-system modeling, the changing coast, groundwater, monsoons, atmospheric chemistry and aerosols, deep-ocean overturning and land-atmosphere interactions.

The course has coherent stopping points within each unit. No further Earth Systems unit is precommitted; expand only for a substantial explanatory problem not better served by a standalone chemistry, geology, ecology or plant biology course. A dedicated chemistry foundation is the next cross-library priority, not a reason to duplicate it inside Earth Systems.

**Metadata convention:** Date tags for a named research publication, observed interval or historical event must say that explicitly; they do not date general physical processes. Place tags come from geographic examples actually discussed in the manuscripts.
