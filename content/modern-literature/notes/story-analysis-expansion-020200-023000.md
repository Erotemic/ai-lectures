## Editorial decisions

- The retelling and its companion are separately playable but sequenced together by local IDs. No mechanical ten-minute splits, and no fixed maximum/minimum words, time, or scene count.
- The plots are told with the ending; the analyses address historical context, fictional technique and disputed interpretations rather than trying to supply missing plot.
- Gatsby's story keeps Nick's viewpoint visible and avoids passing his report off as omniscience. The depiction of Jewish and Black figures is not exempt from analysis because the novel is canonical.
- Orwell's novel is distinguished from both a literal technological forecast and a generic shorthand for all disliked government activity. The question of institutional evidence receives special attention.
- The *Dune* story follows the 1965 novel, not later sequels or films. Herbert's fictive sandworm/spice ecology is not asserted as real environmental science. Daniel Immerwahr's scholarly research is cited for the Quileute connection, rather than assuming all Fremen features map to one present-day culture.
