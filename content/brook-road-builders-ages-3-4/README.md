# Brook Road Builders — Dogs, Trucks, Animals & a Little Scientist

A **separate original episodic children's bedtime-story pack** for ages three to four, primarily age three. It is not a continuation of Willow Bend, and no characters, places, or possessions are shared with that series.

**Premise:** Rex, a brown-and-white puppy, Gus, a young bear with a green cap, and Jack, a little boy who enjoys being a scientist, live along Brook Road. The series follows jobs that change their neighborhood. Talking animals and one human child coexist normally in this fictional world. The machinery behaves like real machinery: different machines do different jobs, and adult builders operate the heavy equipment.

**Season One: Pine Park** — episodes 001000–006000. A vacant field by a lone pine is gradually transformed into a neighborhood park. The park remains part of the world when the machines leave.

**Season Two: The Little Bridge** — episodes 007000–012000. A small pedestrian bridge connects the far side of Pine Park to Green Gate Farm. Rex discovers another puppy, Molly; the friends watch a mixer truck and crane, test a paper bridge at home, and finally walk across. The new path, bridge and farm become permanent locations; trucks take the longer vehicle roads.

**Season Three: Farm Workdays** — episodes 013000–018000. Rex, Gus and Jack visit Molly at Green Gate Farm while Farmer Ruth's red tractor and farm equipment cut, dry, rake, bale and store hay over successive days. Molly learns the path back to Pine Park and sees Pancake Hill for the first time. The farm's hayfield, tractor, stored bales and new two-puppy tail greeting become part of the lasting world.

**Season Four: The Garage with the Red Door** - episodes 019000-024000. Ms. Pam the raccoon mechanic runs a new red-door garage west of Rex's house. Her flatbed recovers Aunt Jo's yellow work van, the shop prepares a new tire and lifts the vehicle, Jack studies toy tire patterns, and a separate service truck visits Farmer Ruth's tractor. Rex's own wooden dump truck receives a tiny home repair. The repaired vehicles, original roads and animals remain part of the continuing town.

**Season Five: The Delivery Route** — episodes 025000–030000. Dan the donkey runs an adult-operated blue box truck from a small parcel shed near the Red Door Garage. Four nesting orange cones arrive at the garage, a rain gauge at Pine Park, and hen feed and a new green wheelbarrow at Green Gate Farm. A liftgate and hand truck do different jobs, the farm road remains for trucks, and the route ends with all packages delivered and Jack sketching the connected roads.
