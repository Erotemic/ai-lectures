# Brook Road Builders — completed 14-season canon and future directions (not spoken)

**The Path to Space is complete as one eighteen-episode arc, 067000–084000, spanning three seasons.** It follows eleven prior seasons, which remain canon unchanged. Season Twelve builds distant coastal Star Harbor; Season Thirteen prepares the crew rocket and sends the Scout robotic delivery mission; Season Fourteen puts the four young friends and two trained adults on the Moon, establishes a small rover research station and returns everyone safely to Brook Road. The spaceflight of young animals and a young child is speculative fiction. See `WORLD_BIBLE.md` and `SPACE_ARC_BIBLE.md` for all continuing facts.

**Potential continuation (none committed):** The lunar rover may send a new discovery or photograph back to Dr. Ada's mission control; Rex and Molly can follow that news while maintaining their farm-and-park routines. The existing town can also continue with smaller-scale construction jobs and genuine vehicle variety. A further crewed flight would require a distinct adult-led project and additional hardware, not a magically reused launch vehicle. Story arcs should have consequences, not reset the world.

# Brook Road Builders — future seasons and story backlog (not spoken)

The completed canon comprises Season One (Pine Park, 001000–006000), Season Two (The Little Bridge, 007000–012000), and Season Three (Farm Workdays, 013000–018000), Season Four (The Garage with the Red Door, 019000–024000), and Season Five (The Delivery Route, 025000–030000), Season Six (After the Rain, 031000–036000), Season Seven (Firehouse Open Day, 037000–042000), Season Eight (Snow Trucks, 043000–048000), Season Nine (Where the Gravel Comes From, 049000–054000), Season Ten (The Big Barn Fix, 055000–060000), and Season Eleven (One Long Road, 061000–066000). All eleven seasons listed here are completed canon; later directions are optional and cannot overwrite earlier places, objects or character relationships. See `WORLD_BIBLE.md` for what is actually established and `SERIES_ATLAS.md` for route/character/vehicle expansion notes.

- **Season Three completed:** **Farm Workdays** — red tractor and hay-making equipment work through an adult-led hay harvest; Molly visits Pine Park for the first time. The farm hayfield is south of the red barn; harvested bales are stored inside.
- **Season Four completed:** **The Garage with the Red Door** — Ms. Pam, a raccoon mechanic, uses a white flatbed tow truck, pressure gauge, lift and separate white mobile service truck. The yellow van and red tractor return to work; Rex's toy dump truck gets a small home repair. The garage is west of the blue-step home at the farm-road junction.
- **Season Five completed:** **The Delivery Route** — Dan the donkey drives a blue enclosed box truck. Deliveries connect the existing garage, park and farm, and a little parcel shed appears west of the garage. The park rain gauge remains ready for the next rainy-weather season.
- **Season Six completed:** **After the Rain** — measured first rainfall, some water soaking into soil and some running toward the street inlet, a street sweeper and drain-cleaning vacuum truck with different jobs, and a cleared culvert and regraveled shoulder on the long farm road. The gauge is empty and ready for another rain.
- **Season Seven completed:** **Firehouse Open Day** — Captain Ann the fox firefighter shows Rex, Gus, Jack and Molly a red engine, a separate ladder truck, gear and a calm practice-water demonstration. Station Lane is drawn on Jack's map; no equipment or emergency issue is left unfinished.
- **Season Eight completed:** **Snow Trucks** — winter road plow and salt spreader, seasonal blade on the familiar farm tractor, Mr. Lee’s snowblower, Jack’s open-snow vs drift observation, and a hand-cleared Little Bridge. No new named adult or uncompleted road job.
- **Season Nine completed:** **Where the Gravel Comes From** — one small park gate repair traces material through crusher, screen, conveyor, finished piles, wheel loader, truck scale and road dump truck. Mr. Fred, adult badger yard manager, is now recurring. No trucks enter Little Bridge.
- **Season Ten completed:** **The Big Barn Fix** — Molly discovers a leak in the existing small wagon-door shelter; Mr. Lee repairs only that area with delivered timber, a braced frame, temporary yellow telehandler, new roofing and controlled runoff. A light shower confirms the shelter stays dry, and Farmer Ruth returns the original green wheelbarrow. The main barn and dry hay remain unchanged.
- **Season Eleven completed:** **One Long Road** — Dan's long vehicle-road trip to the farm contrasts with the puppies' Little Bridge walk. Gus builds a cardboard town; Molly corrects Jack's sketch; Ms. Pam, Captain Ann and Mr. Fred check the existing junctions; Dan and Mr. Lee deliver and mount a modest picture map beside Pine Park's original sign. The neighbors gather for a small picnic. All prior places and objects persist.

**Beyond Season Eleven (optional):** Follow a recurring place or character rather than introducing a machine each time. Possibilities: a puppy-centered farm week, an ordinary night at the garage, the first time Jack repairs his own toy with Ms. Pam, a hay wagon return, or a trip along the long farm road in a grown-up-driven passenger vehicle. These are not committed episodes and need not become a new fixed-length arc.

Future authors should introduce only a few new names at a time, confirm vehicle functions before writing, prefer scenes with a concrete surprise or joke to machine-catalogue monologues, and let at least some chapters center on Rex's dog experience, Gus's comic point of view, or Jack's genuine uncertainty. Keep the spoken word count appropriate to each story rather than a fixed target.
