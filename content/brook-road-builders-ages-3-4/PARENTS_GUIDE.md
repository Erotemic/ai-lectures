# Parent / caregiver guide — Brook Road Builders (not spoken)

The primary intended listener is a three-year-old who enjoys **dogs, other animals, trucks, and construction machinery**. The third protagonist, Jack, is a **little boy scientist**. His curiosity is modelled as noticing, wondering, drawing, and changing his expectations when he sees something new, not as instantly explaining every physical phenomenon.

## Season One: Pine Park

The same friends and location recur for six visits. The machinery names remain consistent and clear: **excavator digs; dump truck delivers and tips; bulldozer pushes; wheel loader scoops and carries; roller compacts**. The large machines are driven by adults, seen from outside the construction fence. The children do their own small-scale playing at home and later walk through the finished park.

| Episode | One optional, simple conversation prompt |
|---|---|
| 001 | Which part of the yellow digger moved when its tracks stayed still? |
| 002 | What happened to the gravel when the truck bed went up? |
| 003 | What is Pancake Hill shaped like? |
| 004 | What kind of print would Rex's paw make in sand? |
| 005 | What did the big round roller do to the path? |
| 006 | Which machine would you like to see again? |

A child may request a favorite machine scene again, skip an episode, ask about a different truck, or want the end of the story with no prompt. No comprehension test is necessary. The written rumbles, crunches and whooshes are narrator-readable and do not require added sound effects or overstimulating sound design. Endings taper down to a quiet familiar scene.

## Why there are sources

- **Machine functions:** Caterpillar's equipment descriptions were consulted to avoid confusing bulldozers, diggers, loaders, trucks and compactors. These are general equipment facts; the fictional site is not an engineering case study and the passages are not operation instructions.
- **Reading/interaction:** AAP and ZERO TO THREE provide general age-three shared-reading context for repetition and optional discussion. They do not establish a quantified or causal learning, behavior, or sleep outcome from these specific audio stories.

Source URLs, authorship, and per-passage rationale records are in `metadata/sources.json` and `metadata/<episode>.json`. These records are not read by TTS.
