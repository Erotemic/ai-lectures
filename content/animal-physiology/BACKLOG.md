# Comparative physiology: further lectures

The first twenty-seven manuscripts are complete and indexed, including a single annual-cycle capstone. Chapters `014000`–`017000` develop endocrine communication; chapters `018000`–`021000` develop immune recognition, comparative defense, and immune tolerance. Continue to determine lecture boundaries from the actual mechanism and explanatory scope; there is no required word count or narration duration.

## Earlier: endocrine physiology

- **014000 — Messages in the Blood:** Bayliss and Starling's secretin experiments, receptor specificity, peptide/steroid/thyroid signals, downstream signaling, hormone clearance, feedback and measurement.
- **015000 — The Meal, the Fast, and the Pancreas:** beta-cell sensing, insulin and glucagon, hepatic glucose production, exercise, incretins, leptin and Toronto insulin research.
- **016000 — A Stress Response Has a History:** sympathetic versus HPA signals, glucocorticoid patterns and feedback, circadian and ultradian dynamics, sampling limitations and ecological costs.
- **017000 — When a Body Changes Its Rules:** anuran and insect metamorphosis, tissue competence, GnRH pulse-frequency experiments and seasonal melatonin duration coding.

## Earlier: immune physiology

- **018000 — The Alarm Before the Army:** barriers, recognition, Toll and TLR4 experiments, vascular inflammation, complement, interferons, and active resolution.
- **019000 — How a Defense Remembers:** receptor rearrangement, clonal selection, MHC-restricted recognition, germinal-center maturation and memory in jawed vertebrates.
- **020000 — More Than One Way to Defend a Body:** fly wasp-egg encapsulation, Dicer-2, sea urchin receptor expansions, bumblebee priming and lamprey VLR-based adaptive immunity.
- **021000 — When Defense Must Stand Down:** thymic selection, AIRE, FOXP3 Tregs, gut IgA-microbiota coexistence, efferocytosis and immune costs.

## Completed in this iteration: reproduction and life history

- **022000 — The Price of a Gamete:** meiosis, anisogamy, sperm and egg allocation, timing, and sequential reproductive-role changes in fish.
- **023000 — One Sperm, One Egg, One Beginning:** gamete-specific recognition, IZUMO1/JUNO, sea urchin bindin, egg calcium signals, polyspermy and first embryonic divisions.
- **024000 — Eggs, Pouches, and Placentas:** embryo nutrition and gas exchange in laid eggs, maternal pregnancies, independent vertebrate origins of live bearing and seahorse paternal brooding.
- **025000 — Who Feeds the Young?:** parental care beyond mammals, oxygen supply in goby nests, poison-frog alkaloid provisioning, burying beetle experiments and allocation tradeoffs.
- **026000 — A Lifetime to Reproduce:** reproductive timing, semelparity and iteroparity, seasonal pools and killifish diapause, Hamilton/Williams/Kirkwood models of senescence.

## Completed integrative capstone and stopping point

- **027000 — A Year in One Body: The Elephant Seal's Physiological Budget:** One continuous case following a representative female through two foraging trips, a molt, a breeding fast and lactation, linking tissue oxygen stores, dive reflex, hydrodynamics, body condition, sleep, endocrine response, renal water conservation, delayed implantation and lifetime reproductive output. The animal is a composite of field populations, never a fabricated individual record.
- **Course stopping point:** The 27-lecture course now covers core functional systems and an integrative synthesis. Further specialized deep dives are optional and should only be added when they develop a new explanatory problem, not as routine indefinite expansion.
