# The Dynamic Earth: Plates, Quakes, and Volcanoes

**Status:** nine completed standalone audio-first lectures, numbered 001000–009000. Independent Science and Nature pack.

This first unit focuses on solid-Earth mechanics: empirical evidence for plate tectonics, seismic interior inference, plate driving forces, ridge construction, subduction, earthquake rupture, recorded seismic waves, mantle and crustal melting, and eruption hazards.
The primary audience is curious adult listeners; every lecture begins from observations and explains its specialist terminology. No prior science course is required.

## Authoring contract

Each Markdown manuscript has one spoken region, a scientific companion with formulas or calculations where helpful, readings, and review questions. The metadata sidecar contains searchable concepts, source IDs and supported geography/date associations. The navigation file provides one complete course and five listening routes. No audio is included.

## Why this course exists

The companion Earth Systems course emphasizes atmospheric, oceanic and biosphere cycles. This course gives solid-Earth dynamics its own mechanical development rather than recapping the chronology covered in Deep Time.

## Next expansion

The logical continuation is the surface and rock-record arc: isostasy and mountain building, metamorphism and rock-cycle chemistry, sediment transport and landscapes, relative geological time, geochronology, and structural inference. Local geohazard case studies can follow.
