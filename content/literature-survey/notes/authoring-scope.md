# Literary introductions and narrative readings: exclusive authoring scope

This pack owns new survey introductions; it does not own or replace the longer
studies in other packs. Namespace `literature-survey` was checked against the
current source inventories on 16 November 2026 and was unused.

Indexed introductions:

- `001000`: Boccaccio's Decameron: the frame, representative tales, and the book's form.
- `002000`: Romance of the Three Kingdoms: the whole narrative arc and competing loyalties.
- `003000`: Shahnameh: legendary kings, heroic families, and the end of a royal world.

Ancient-epics expansion indexed 2026-11-16:

- `006000`: Virgil's Aeneid, twelve-book story and interpretive ending.
- `007000`: Apollonius's Argonautica, four-book story focused on Jason and Medea.
- `008000`: Ovid's Metamorphoses, selective linked treatment of fifteen books.
- `009000`: Nala and Damayanti, complete episode inside the Mahabharata.

These are newly authored, standalone lectures and **not** modifications to the
World History source track. The Egyptian Tale of Two Brothers and other existing
works in the ancient route are reused, not duplicated. The new lectures are
listed in both the Ancient Epics route and Compact Introductions route; source
records and authored claim locators remain local to this pack.

Living oral-traditions expansion indexed 2026-11-16:

- `010000`: Gunditjmara: Budj Bim and the Living Country, a sourced
  commentary on publicly shared Indigenous knowledge. This is a living
  oral tradition and landscape history, not a surviving ancient text,
  a complete song cycle, or a community-authorized performance.

- `011000`: Klamath: Llao, Skell, and the Heart at Crater Lake. Original
  commentary on a living oral tradition, based on identifiable and carefully
  separated publicly documented versions. Direct review of Robert David's
  Klamath-language publication (Barker 1963 pp. 71–75) remains a follow-up
  before a definitive source-led adaptation could be asserted. No traditional
  performance authorization claimed.

Optional future ideas, not required organization work:

- `004000`: a Water Margin synopsis, if commissioned; the current route uses
  its existing opening narrative and textual-history essay.
- `005000`: a shorter Journey to the West synopsis, if commissioned; the
  current route links existing introductory/context material to the complete
  six-part survey and the unfinished expanded retelling.

IDs 004000 and 005000 are working reservations, not playable entries. Recheck
the current inventory before assigning them. A manuscript enters
`track.json` only with usable narration and source records. References to
historical events and the novels' narrative inventions remain distinct.

The independent expanded Journey to the West project is outside this pack's
scope. Finishing its chapters 13–100 is not a prerequisite for reorganizing
the already complete overview and the currently available expanded chapters.
