# Compact literary introductions: exclusive authoring scope

This pack owns new survey introductions; it does not own or replace the longer
studies in other packs. Namespace `literature-survey` was checked against the
current source inventories on 16 November 2026 and was unused.

Indexed introductions:

- `001000`: Boccaccio's Decameron: the frame, representative tales, and the book's form.
- `002000`: Romance of the Three Kingdoms: the whole narrative arc and competing loyalties.
- `003000`: Shahnameh: legendary kings, heroic families, and the end of a royal world.

Optional future ideas, not required organization work:

- `004000`: a Water Margin synopsis, if commissioned; the current route uses
  its existing opening narrative and textual-history essay.
- `005000`: a shorter Journey to the West synopsis, if commissioned; the
  current route links existing introductory/context material to the complete
  six-part survey and the unfinished expanded retelling.

IDs 004000 and 005000 are working reservations, not playable entries. Recheck
the current inventory before assigning them. A manuscript enters
`track.json` only with usable narration and source records. References to
historical events and the novels' narrative inventions remain distinct.

The independent expanded Journey to the West project is outside this pack's
scope. Finishing its chapters 13–100 is not a prerequisite for reorganizing
the already complete overview and the currently available expanded chapters.
