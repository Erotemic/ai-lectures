# Optional future extensions

The six-lecture introductory course is complete. Further lectures should arise from an actual learning need rather than from pressure to indefinitely extend this route.

Possible deeper directions:

- The Lie group–Lie algebra correspondence with covering groups and global integration proofs.
- Peter–Weyl theory, matrix coefficient orthogonality and Fourier analysis on nonabelian compact groups.
- The universal enveloping algebra and the Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem.
- Root-system axioms, Cartan matrices, Dynkin diagrams, the Weyl character formula and a proof of the highest-weight classification.
- More representation examples: SU(3) weight diagrams, tensor-product rules, and Lie groups in quantum mechanics and gauge theory.
- Lie algebra cohomology, deformation theory, and computational/formal proof approaches.

The existing abstract algebra, topology, linear algebra and category-theory tracks contain significant overlapping prerequisites and advanced consequences; consult those before adding more introductions.
