## Editorial review before each extension

- Does the new episode **start** from the actual previous state and finish with a visible lasting difference?
- Is the immediate problem understandable without the last episode?
- Does the ending itself satisfy, without a high-stress teaser?
- Is the cast small enough for one narrator? Are character names clearly attached to actions rather than voice acting?
- Are optional caregiver prompts and references separated from narration with evidence limits?
- Are the timeline and geography consistent with the world bible?

No fixed episode length; write what the story requires. Avoid mechanically inserting a developmental theme. New seasons may have more or fewer than six stories.
