# Children's Bedtime Stories — Episodic (Ages 3–4)

**Track ID:** `childrens-bedtime-stories-episodic-ages-3-4`  
**Series:** *Willow Bend and the Little Lights*; ten completed seasons  
**Primary listener:** age three; intended age range: three to four  
**Performance:** one consistent TTS narrator; no character acting, music, effects, or images needed  
**Order:** listen in sequence for the full arc, but each episode supplies enough context to listen alone  
**Length:** determined by each story's narrative needs; no minute or word-count target

This is a **separate pack** from the existing collection of standalone bedtime stories. Here, the people, places, relationships, and shared objects remain changed from one episode to the next. An evening comes to a satisfying close every time; the listener is never left in distress until tomorrow.

## Continuity contract for future agents

Read [WORLD_BIBLE.md](WORLD_BIBLE.md) before authoring another episode. It defines the canonical relationships, map, spatial layout, recurrent objects, and season-one end state. Read [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md) for possible directions, not fixed obligations. Do **not** rewrite earlier character histories, magically straighten the lantern, forget Kim's preferences, or reset the map. A future episode can develop any of those details intentionally, but must show the change on the page.

A new episode should offer a natural small recap, one locally satisfying story, a calm endpoint, and one or two earned additions to the shared world. It can be funny, mysterious, tender, or quiet. Avoid introducing more characters than an age-three listener can distinguish by context when spoken in a single voice.

After many further weeks of adult care, the Story House's real garden changes substantially without impossible instant growth. The spring radishes have already been harvested at the appropriate point, and the two sunflower stems grow tall: one blooms by the season end, while the other remains budded. The original LITTLE GROWING GARDEN sign is moved a little along the dry path to remain readable. The first garden drawing and an interim sketch remain in the original folder, with a final, new **THE GARDEN TODAY** drawing added alongside them. Amy adds a single yellow flower dot inside the existing garden square of her **real map**, preserving the western Market Street bus flap.
