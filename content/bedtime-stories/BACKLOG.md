## Questions for future authoring

- Do children and caregivers prefer an explicitly soothing ending, or simply a
  resolved story that settles naturally?
- Which recurring characters become familiar without making individual
  installments dependent on previous episodes?
- How does one consistent TTS narrator perform on brief direct speech versus
  reported speech, repetition, alliteration, and embedded sound words?
- Should later batches include household routines, weather, imaginary worlds,
  and family structures beyond the first set?
- What audio-level or pronunciation fixes should be made **only after** a
  real listening review rather than inferred from the manuscript?

Do not impose a fixed story duration. The age range is **3–4**, primarily age 3.
These are editorial questions, not a validator checklist.

### Next evaluation questions

- Does the TTS narrator handle brief dialogue, repeated refrains and softer
  endings naturally? Keep all voices in a consistent narrator register.
- Do caregivers find the parent reading useful without wanting lectures in
  the child's narration?
- Is the balance of gentle conflict and settling suitable for the individual child?
- A future story could explore saying no to unwanted affection or contacting
  a trusted adult, but only with child-safety review; toy sharing is not a substitute.
