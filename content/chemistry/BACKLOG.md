# Chemistry: future course development

The twenty-eight indexed lectures cover chemistry foundations, reaction principles,
organic structure and mechanisms, polymer chemistry, and analytical measurement.

## Materials chemistry and phases

- Develop phase diagrams, crystal growth and nucleation, glasses, ceramics, and the way materials acquire structure while being processed.
- Explain surfaces and interfaces: wetting, adsorption, emulsions, colloids, and the relation between catalysts and surface chemistry.
- Expand corrosion and electrochemical interfaces, passivation, and the limits of idealized cell models.

## Advanced organic and biochemistry connections

- Aromatic substitution, enolate and carbon-carbon bond construction, multistep synthesis, and chemical selectivity where the molecular narrative warrants deeper treatment.
- Photochemistry, chemical ecology and biologically mediated reactions, crosslinking Cell Biology, Earth Systems and The Living World without duplicating them.

## Analytical chemistry specializations worth considering later

- Isotope-ratio measurement and trace-element geochemistry, and how standards, contamination and isotope fractionation affect the inference.
- Imaging chemical composition with microscopy and spectroscopy; spatial resolution versus chemical selectivity.
- Chemometrics and multivariate spectra: uncertainty, model leakage, reference datasets and false confidence in library matches.

Let depth and explanatory need determine lecture length and unit structure.
Add chronology and geography only for documented events actually discussed in narration.
