# Chemistry: From Atoms to Reactions

**Status:** sixty-two completed audio-first lectures (001000-062000). This is an independent pack in Science and Nature.

The course begins with matter, bonds, solutions, reaction quantities, thermodynamics, kinetics, equilibrium and electrochemistry. The organic chemistry extension builds carbon structures, recognizes functional groups, reasons about stereochemistry, follows electron-pair mechanisms and reaches macromolecules and material behavior. The analytical chemistry sequence explains how sampling, calibration, spectroscopy, separations and mass measurements support defensible chemical identifications. The materials sequence develops phase equilibrium, crystal growth, glasses, ceramics, wetting, colloids, corrosion and processing-dependent properties. The electronic-matter-and-light unit then develops energy bands, controlled doping, semiconductor junctions, LEDs, photovoltaic devices and light-driven molecular and biological reactions. The bioinorganic and metabolic extension then examines why enzymes recruit metals, how heme, iron-sulfur and B12 cofactors work, how ATP and electron carriers sustain chemistry, why nitrogen fixation is difficult, and how respiration couples redox to ATP. The organic synthesis extension develops selectivity, aromatic directing effects, enolates, carbon-carbon bond construction, cycloaddition, cross-coupling, protection, asymmetric synthesis and two complete retrosynthetic case studies. Each lecture is standalone with a spoken manuscript and an unspoken scientific companion.
