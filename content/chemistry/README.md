# Chemistry: From Atoms to Reactions

**Status:** thirteen completed substantive audio-first lectures (001000–013000), organized into foundations and chemical-change units. This is one independent pack in the Science and Nature collection.

The course proceeds from observable identity and atomic structure to bonding, solutions, reaction quantities and energetics, then to how reactions actually unfold over time, how reversible chemical systems approach equilibrium, how acidity and buffering work, and how electron-transfer reactions produce or consume electrical work. Every lecture has a self-contained spoken manuscript and a separately readable scientific companion.

### Guided routes

`navigation.json` retains the original main collection ID, `chemistry-foundations`, and expands it into a thirteen-lecture path. The original shorter routes are retained. Three deep dives cover **reaction rates and equilibrium**, **acidity and buffers**, and **batteries and electrolysis**.

### Evidence, discovery, and authored content

Every manuscript contains one extractable spoken region with no equations or reference bookkeeping interrupting the narration, and an unspoken scientific companion containing the actual formulas, worked examples, readings and review questions. Lecture-specific sidecars record scientific claims with exact spoken anchors and source IDs; `metadata/sources.json` holds those bibliographic entries. Descriptive topic paths identify specific concepts. Geographic and chronology metadata appear only where supported by episodes actually discussed, notably Volta and Daniell's cells and early water electrolysis.

For continuing expansion, consult `BACKLOG.md`. This content-only pack does not include audio, image files, runtime pack exports or changes outside `content/chemistry/`.
