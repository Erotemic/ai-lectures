# Foundational mathematics: gaps after Continuation 32

This track now teaches elementary proof technique through epsilon arguments and compactness. Follow-on core mathematics subjects should be selected by unmet prerequisites, not by the novelty of an advanced theorem.

## Next high-impact core foundations

- **Construction and properties of real numbers:** integers to rationals, completeness, Dedekind cuts or Cauchy completions, Archimedean property, supremum proofs, dense sets; align with Analysis 001000-004000 rather than repeating its current survey.
- **Elementary number theory:** divisibility, Euclidean algorithm, Bezout identity, prime factorization, congruences, Chinese remainder theorem; foundational before discrete algorithms and abstract algebra.
- **Probability from finite sample spaces:** events, conditional probability, Bayes' rule, random variables, expectation, variance, and independence before sigma-algebra formalization; audit existing probability material first.
- **Elementary abstract structures:** groups, rings, fields from concrete integers and permutations before module and categorical language; audit the existing abstract algebra track before authoring.
- **Problem-solving and proof practice:** worked proof repairs, quantifier-order errors, false converses, missing assumptions, and genuinely rigorous exercises at increasing difficulty. Preserve these as mathematical problems, not arbitrary writing rubrics.

## Scope boundaries

Do not duplicate the existing constructive proof-assistant lectures in `tts-logic-types`, advanced combinatorics in `tts-discrete-mathematics`, or the advanced compactness theorems already in `tts-analysis`. This track supplies the vocabulary and simplest reliable derivations. Advanced lectures should continue to state their own specialized assumptions.
