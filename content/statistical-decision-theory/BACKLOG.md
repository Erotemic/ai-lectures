# Future optional extensions

The six-lecture foundation is self-contained. Add further material only for a specific teaching need, rather than extending the route indefinitely.

Potential advanced subjects:

- Bayesian robustness and sensitivity to prior or likelihood misspecification.
- Formal complete-class theorems, least-favorable priors and additional admissibility examples.
- Sequential decision theory, stopping rules, contextual bandits and value of experimentation.
- Importance sampling and sequential Monte Carlo, with diagnostics for weight degeneracy.
- Simulation-based calibration, adaptive MCMC theory, and more developed proofs of geometric ergodicity.
- Bayesian experimental design and causal decision-making.

Existing coverage: conditional probability, likelihood, posterior updating and Markov chains are already treated in `probability-measure`. Keep any new lectures focused on their additional mathematical substance.
