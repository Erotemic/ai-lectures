## Editorial position

These are independently listenable, critical audio essays for fans rather than a completeness-first episode guide. Individual lectures may spoil major plot developments; the four Essentials essays limit their scope. Spoken sections are marked for narration, with non-spoken research notes and claim anchors in `metadata/`. Source-based production history is distinguished from on-screen events and critical interpretation. No copyrighted footage, imagery or audio is included.

This track has a deliberate conclusion in `027000`. Additional SG-1 specials or other Stargate-series tracks are optional future projects, not open items in this plan.
