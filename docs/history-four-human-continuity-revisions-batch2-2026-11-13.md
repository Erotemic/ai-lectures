# Four historical portrait revisions: Pythagoras, Hypatia, Runge and Kutta

This cumulative-state text overlay revises four previously completed historical portraits. It preserves the remaining 38 lecture entries and all four mathematical companions.

## Editorial work

- **Pythagoras (040000):** embed the migration from Samos, early hostile testimonies, religious practice, women in the community, and civic conflict. Do not attribute later mathematical results directly to the historical Pythagoras.
- **Hypatia (042000):** develop Theon's educational environment, Synesius's letters and grief, the hydrometer request, and the public relationship with Orestes. Preserve the distinction between Synesius's literary self-presentation, later testimony, and Hypatia's missing words.
- **Runge (031000):** integrate family life, Aimée du Bois-Reymond, colleagues, music, cycling, and the impact of World War I. Connect those scenes to laboratory work and the Göttingen years.
- **Kutta (032000):** integrate orphanhood, wide reading, Cambridge and Munich, Pfeiffer's biographical recollections, teaching, and work with engineers. Reorder the closing so Kutta's death occurs after his working life and mathematical conclusion.

Each manuscript was reviewed for abrupt shifts in time and for section boundaries that separate an anecdote from its mathematical consequence. Claim anchors were reconciled with the revised prose, preserving their original source ids. No audio or binary assets are packaged.
