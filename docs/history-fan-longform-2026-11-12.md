# Ky Fan: long-form History lecture promotion

- **Baseline:** Robert Schatten long-form repository snapshot, with 13 playable
  portraits and 15 archived short biographies.
- **Promotion:** `012000` becomes the fourteenth playable long-form portrait;
  `stubs/012000-fan.md` and `stubs/metadata/012000.json` are retired after
  extraction, leaving 14 archived biographies.
- **Spoken length:** 5,758 whitespace-delimited words; approximately
  39.7 minutes at 145 words per minute. TTS audio was not generated.
- **Historical spine:** Hangzhou and Peking University; Paris/Fréchet and
  CNRS; IAS 1945–47; Notre Dame and later U.S. appointments; UCSB; Academia
  Sinica; 1999 Ky and Yu-Fen Fan Endowment.
- **Mathematical spine:** singular values and spectral distribution; Ky Fan
  partial-sum norms; crossing and comparable examples; Hermitian versus
  two-frame variational principles; triangle inequality and failure of
  individual singular-value additivity; weak majorization and gauge-norm
  duality; Fan's 1949/1950 response to Weyl, 1951 compact-operator paper;
  1952–1961 fixed-point and minimax contributions.
- **Source research:** 16 new source records including original PNAS papers,
  ILAS obituary, AMS gift account, Academia Sinica, and peer-reviewed
  mathematical references. The legacy generic sources remain in the catalog
  for archived materials but are not used as authority for this promotion.
- **Attribution:** Weyl's July 1949 paper predates Fan's 1949/1950 response;
  von Neumann's matrix gauge theory and pre-Fan majorization are separate;
  1929 KKM predates Fan's extensions; Davis–Kahan is only a later use case.
- **Packaging:** Text-only incremental ZIP. Zip extraction does not delete
  tracked stubs; retire them with the explicit `git rm` command.
