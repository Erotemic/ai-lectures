# John von Neumann: long-form History lecture promotion

- **Baseline:** the Frigyes Riesz long-form working repository, cumulatively
  containing the prior History promotions.
- **Promotion:** `010000` from `stubs/` into the playable History track.
  Other 16 archived sketches remain unchanged.
- **Spoken length:** 5,152 words, an estimated 35.5 minutes at
  145 words per minute. Actual length depends on voice and synthesis speed;
  no audio was generated or auditioned.
- **Biographical content:** education in Budapest, Zurich and Gottingen;
  mathematical foundations; the 1932 quantum foundations monograph;
  operator rings and collaboration with Francis Murray; 1937 matrix inequalities;
  mean ergodic theorem and its distinct relation to Birkhoff; minimax games;
  the EDVAC team and priority dispute; IAS computing and national-security work.
- **Mathematical examples:** two-state quantum observable, continuous-spectrum
  multiplication operator and spectral projection, coherent superposition vs.
  mixed density operator, operator-algebra center, type II-one dimension,
  singular-value norms, trace pairing (22 vs. 14), principal-angle projections
  (3/4 overlap vs. 1/2 distance), rotating averages, and zero-sum game minimax.
- **Attribution:** Hilbert-space and measurement development reflects several
  researchers; the Murray--von Neumann factors are coauthored; the mean and
  pointwise ergodic theorems differ; the EDVAC design was collaborative and
  early stored-program concepts predate von Neumann's involvement. Bell's
  critique of 1932 hidden-variable assumptions is noted in the companion.
- **Readings:** 18 entries, including primary publications,
  scholarly mathematical exposition, IAS biographies, and historical
  computing research; each uses an exact source-catalog identifier.
- **Delivery:** text-only incremental ZIP. Explicit `git rm` is required
  for the two superseded stub files; extraction alone does not remove them.
