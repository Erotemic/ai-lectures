# Historical portrait expansion: Joseph Fourier and Hypatia of Alexandria

## Scope

Adds two standalone History lectures, `041000` and `042000`, to the cumulative 40-portrait snapshot. All earlier lectures and their identifiers are unchanged; the catalog now contains 42 portraits.

- **Fourier:** 4,780 extracted spoken words (33.0 minutes at 145 WPM). Heat conduction, conservation laws, separated modes, orthogonality, trigonometric series, smoothing, numerical examples, and his 1824 inquiry into terrestrial temperatures. The biography covers Revolution, Egypt, Grenoble, academy debates and publication.
- **Hypatia:** 4,700 extracted spoken words (32.4 minutes at 145 WPM). Alexandrian teaching, Diophantus, Apollonius, Ptolemy, astronomy, Synesius, and the surviving records of her death. Modernized mathematical exercises are explicitly distinguished from her lost original work.

The display-only companions include the exact equations, proofs or calculations, attribution boundaries, and historical-source guidance. They are not part of TTS narration.
