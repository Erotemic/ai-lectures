# History portraits: human narrative and continuity revision, batch 4

Revised four standalone narrations in the operator/variational history sequence.
The added biographies develop the people through primary recollections, academic
memoirs and historical records, introducing new scenes where their work and
institutions were changing. The existing mathematical teaching and the display-only
companions remain intact.

### Focus by lecture

- Courant: youth and tutoring, the Hilbert circle, war and family, expulsion,
  migration, mentorship and institutional tensions.
- Fischer: Vienna's musical household, the geographic context of his 1907 work,
  mathematical friendship with Emmy Noether, and a family life interrupted by war.
- Hilbert: friendships with Minkowski and Hurwitz, the Göttingen seminar culture,
  his support for Noether, illness, and the destruction of his academic community.
- Riesz: the quiet humor reported by Lorch, teaching and changing manuscript
  plans, work with assistants, the 1936 Budapest rejection, and wartime persecution.

Sources for specific additions are included in the shared `metadata/sources.json`
and anchored in individual `metadata/006000.json` through `metadata/009000.json`.
All changes are text only; no audio or image assets are generated.
