# TTS lecture-opening audit — 11 November 2026

This editorial pass addresses the first minute of a standalone recording. It
uses the actual text inside the spoken markers, not the presence of a title or
an `Introduction` heading, as the criterion.

## Scope and findings

- Inventory: 156 G2 transcripts and 27 G3 transcripts in the working snapshot.
  All 183 contain a spoken opening. The observed problem is **abrupt conceptual
  entry**, not an absent spoken-text section.
- Several recordings state a theorem, a norm constant, or a new collection of
  operator hypotheses before a listener has seen an example that gives those
  objects a purpose.
- Two openings invoked the earlier course sequence rather than establishing
  their own context: G3 `011000` and G2 `039000`. G2 `072000` similarly opened
  by referring to the course's finite-dimensional theorem. These have been
  rewritten as standalone entry points.
- The foundations through G2 `038000`, much of G2 `083000–106000`, and the
  recent G3 `024000–025000` work already have comparatively developed motivating
  examples. They were retained in this pass.
