## Documentary focus

- **Germain:** the revolutionary Paris household; the remembered story of private study; correspondence with Gauss and her intervention for his safety; the institutional barriers surrounding the Academy prize; Fourier and her family circle.
- **Mirzakhani:** her own interviews about reading and childhood aspirations; school confidence and her friendship with Roya Beheshti; the role of supportive educators; mathematical drawing, family life, and her response to public honors.
- **Lovelace:** primary letters about childhood mechanical experiments; the De Morgan mathematical course and its frustrations; documented collaborative work with Babbage; family and health constraints.
- **Galois:** school records, family bereavement, republican involvement, prison conditions, and the uncertain personal circumstances of the duel; the mathematical correspondence with Chevalier.

The source ledger includes new interview, archival and scholarly references, and the four historical-claim lists include exact anchors for the new scenes. Mathematical companions retain the pre-existing calculations; concise new source sections are display-only.

For application, use this overlay after the Newton–Fourier human-portrait revision and after any prior overlay that added portraits up through `042000`. No binary media are included.
