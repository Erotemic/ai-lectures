# Robert Schatten: long-form History lecture promotion

- **Baseline:** John von Neumann long-form working repository, after Frigyes Riesz and earlier History promotions.
- **Promotion:** `011000` moved from archived short-portrait status into the playable History track. Other **15** sketches remain archived.
- **Spoken length:** 5,736 words; approximately 39.6 minutes at 145 words per minute. The duration is an estimate. No audio was rendered or listened to.
- **Mathematical through-line:** the 2D identity tensor has injective / Hilbert / projective sizes 1 / sqrt(2) / 2; this extends to operator / Hilbert–Schmidt / trace norms. Develops projective tensor decomposition, two endpoint norm completions, Schatten p ideal membership, Hölder estimates and duality, and modern low-rank approximation as an application.
- **Biographical context:** Rubin/Robert Schatten's 1933 master's thesis under Stefan Banach, 1936/1938 documented migration, Columbia work with Francis Murray, 1942–43 injury, joint work with Nelson Dunford and John von Neumann, the 1950 and 1960 monographs, Kansas and Hunter College teaching, and family losses in the Holocaust.
- **Priority:** von Neumann's 1937 matrix symmetric-gauge characterization is distinct; *Cross-Space* I (1946) is Schatten alone and parts II/III (1946/1948) are joint with von Neumann. Later work by Grothendieck, Ky Fan, Eckart, Young, Mirsky, and Davis–Kahan is distinguished from Schatten's original results.
