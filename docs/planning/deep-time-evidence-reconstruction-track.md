# Deep Time — reconstructing what happened, and how we know

**Status:** New curriculum plan, 14 November 2026. Thirty-two substantive lectures delivered (`001000` through `032000`); the initial curriculum is complete. This track complements *World History*, the *How We Know* archaeology strand, *The Physical World*, and *The World Reconsidered*. Its identity is a chronological reconstruction from observable traces, reaching backward as far as current methods allow.

## The question

What can be reconstructed about the past when there were no witnesses, no written records, and eventually no surviving direct images? Start with today's measurements and work backward through physics, chemistry, geology, evolution, paleoanthropology, and archaeology. At every transition explain both what we believe happened and how the evidence lets us infer it. Preserve uncertainty at the level it actually belongs: a well-measured spectrum can support a strong historical explanation while leaving the underlying microphysics unknown.

**Never imply that all historical reconstructions are merely guesses.** A conclusion supported by independent, predictive tests is stronger than a speculative narrative. Equally, do not make the small formal uncertainty in a fitted parameter stand in for uncertainty about the entire model. Precise, provisional, and unknown are all legitimate statuses, sometimes within the same sentence.

## Lecture architecture and evidence discipline

Every substantive lecture should develop a continuous story for listening, supported by a display-only evidence ledger that separates:

1. **Trace / observation** — a detector reading, spectral feature, isotope ratio, mapped geological layer, dated artifact, genomic sequence, or other surviving material; what calibration or processing intervened?
2. **Dating** — observed quantity to inferred age, including clock assumptions, calibration, uncertainties, and the difference between relative and absolute dating.
3. **Reconstruction** — what mechanism or historical sequence the traces support; competing causal paths and where assumptions enter.
4. **Alternatives** — historically important rejected explanations, live minority research programs, and speculative proposals are explicitly distinguished. For each, explain why it does or does not pass a discriminating test.
5. **Confidence and revisions** — qualitative assessment tied to independent observations, selection effects, model sensitivity, and known disagreements; questions whose resolution could change the story.

The ledger is an analytical teaching aid, not a numerical “truth score.” Avoid arbitrary percentages for confidence. Acceptable vocabulary: **directly measured**, **strongly supported reconstruction**, **precise conditional model inference**, **actively disputed inference**, **viable but weakly constrained proposal**, **contradicted by major evidence**, and **presently unknown**. Attach confidence to a particular claim, not to an entire discipline.

**Historical time and model time.** An age quoted as “13.8 billion years” depends on cosmological assumptions; “4.54 billion years” uses radiometric systems and a different chain of calibration; “the first known fossils” gives a lower bound on the existence of a lineage, not its origin time. Avoid claiming a unified precision merely because all epochs share one timeline graphic. Dates in the plan are approximate orientation, not new claims of priority or a substitute for researching the source of each anchor.

**Editorial tone.** Develop discoveries as investigations by people and institutions using specific apparatus and constraints. Do not introduce alternatives merely to be provocative, or blur their different evidentiary statuses. No spoken discussion of repository maintenance, course planning, generation models, or relative lecture order. Keep each recording self-contained for a listener who has its stated prerequisites.

## First lecture evidence scope (001000)

The delivered lecture uses COBE/FIRAS for thermal spectrum, Planck for parameterized age and composition, the PDG and a 2026 light-element review for BBN, the 2024 Dark Energy Survey for cosmic time dilation, SH0ES for a calibrated local Hubble value, and DESI DR2 with 2026 analysis to present the evolving-dark-energy question as unresolved. Current research findings are dated explicitly in the manuscript and source sidecar so the model discussion can be revised while the epistemic framework remains evergreen.

The lecture distinguishes: raw observable → processed quantity → physical reconstruction → model extrapolation. It describes the hot dense phase as strongly supported, not equally speculative with an origin story. It treats an exact beginning, an inflation mechanism, a bouncing predecessor, and microscopic dark-sector identities as open or speculative, as appropriate. It explicitly contrasts hypotheses already in serious conflict with major observations (classical steady state, naive tired light) with live alternative models under investigation.

## Integration and boundaries

- Register `deep-time` as its own audio track with isolated cache and transcript IDs. Do not reuse the World History 200000–299000 archaeology range or modify that track's manifests.
- Lecture narrative includes evidence assessment without reducing the topic to a philosophical introduction; teach the physics/geology/biology with concrete mechanisms and measurements.
- Reuse relevant World History and portrait lectures through conceptual links in future catalog metadata, not copied scripts.
- New lectures receive one numbered manuscript, one per-lecture source/claim sidecar, and entries in `track.json` and `metadata/sources.json`. Only delivered work goes in `track.json`; proposed IDs live here.
- The sources catalog records specific primary experiments and review papers with accessible links and access labels. Claim status distinguishes reference recorded from source-passage checked. Do not represent external verification as Lean proof or automated historical fact checking.
- When the research changes, update a named result and its source, not an entire narrative merely to follow the news. Separate research snapshot dates from the timeless logical distinction between observation and inference.
