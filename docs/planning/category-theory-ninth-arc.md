## Scope and correctness boundaries

- Distinguish exactness, acyclicity, contractibility, quasi-isomorphism and literal chain isomorphism. These properties have different strengths.
- State the base ring, module handedness, grading convention, and degree placement in every Tor or Ext computation.
- Projective resolutions of modules compute Tor in module categories with enough projectives. General unbounded complexes require the appropriate derived replacements.
- Dold–Kan applies to nonnegatively graded complexes in an abelian category and simplicial objects of that category; do not advertise the basic equivalence as an unbounded theorem.
- Stable infinity-categorical results require pointedness, finite limits and colimits, and the stable equivalence between pushouts and pullbacks. Do not infer all higher coherence from a triangulated homotopy category alone.
