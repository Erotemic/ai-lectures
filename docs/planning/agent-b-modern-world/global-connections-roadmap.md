## Completed: political revolutions and industrial transformation

- **011000 — Who Is the People?** Treat sovereignty as contested political membership, not a succession of great-man declarations. Compare the different social coalitions, legal exclusions and institutional outcomes across North America, France and Iberian America. Distinguish the founding document's claims from actual participation.
- **012000 — Haiti Made Freedom.** Develop the Haitian Revolution as an autonomous and internally contested process: 1791 revolt; 1793–94 emancipation; Louverture's military and labor politics; the 1802 expedition; 1804 independence; the political problem of land, labor and legal citizenship; and the coercive 1825 recognition debt. Preserve explicit caution about Bois Caïman accounts and the legal scope of the 1802 French law.
- **013000 — The Factory and Its World.** Contrast skilled Indian textiles with Britain's changing machinery, wages, coal, ecological resource constraints, global cotton and labor. Present Allen's factor-price explanation, Pomeranz's comparative thesis and slavery/industrialization arguments as competing and partly complementary hypotheses, not consensus settled by rhetoric.
- **014000 — A City Built to Work.** Follow women and children's paid and unpaid work, workplace reform and enforcement, workers' political organization, contested urban-mortality evidence and environmental costs. Distinguish legal dates from actual working conditions and growth averages from lived distributions.

### Batch-wide attribution protocol

The user prioritizes unusually explicit provenance. Each source entry links directly to institutional, original documentary, museum or academic material rather than a search-results page. Each narrated paragraph has a corresponding display-only source ledger and structured sidecar claim record, including precise section/title locators and a limitation note. Source access scope is recorded honestly: **a publisher abstract is not equivalent to reviewing the full paper or book**; a primary document describes the author's view, not the empirical truth of the entire event. The reader must be able to follow from prose to source URL without reverse-engineering a general reading list.
