# Agent A - Time, Testimony, Language, and Ideas (032000-035000)

## Delivered unit

Four complete independent audio-first manuscripts continuing the fiction school-of-thought program. The sequence builds from techniques of ordering and perceiving into the social distribution of meaning and ethical/philosophical argument. These are related problems, not a new unified doctrine.

- **032000, Narrative Time:** Genette's order, duration and frequency, using an invented flood chronology to test reader knowledge, scene weight and repetition. The narrative may remain chronological if that arrangement best serves the work.
- **033000, Unreliable Narration:** Booth, Phelan and Shen; reporting, interpreting and evaluating, with an invented envelope case that separates deliberate deceit from honest error and limited focalization.
- **034000, Translation and Multilingual Voice:** Achebe, Ngugi, Anzaldua, and Venuti. A house-sale scene tests four different ways to translate or leave an utterance in context. Emphasizes audience and research rather than tokenizing language.
- **035000, Novel of Ideas:** Murdoch's moral attention, Coetzee's fictional philosophical lectures, and Le Guin's institutional speculation. An invented municipal warning-system debate tests three defensible alternatives and their personal costs.

## Editorial constraints and evidence

- No global word or duration targets. The subject determines the length; narration counts in the manifest are measurements.
- Each manuscript contains exactly one spoken region and display-only companion materials.
- Explicit historical or bibliographic assertions have registered source IDs and in-text anchors.
- All fictional cases are original demonstrations, not alleged episodes from the cited authors' lives or books.
- The treatment of unreliable narration is about formal literary representation and does not purport to establish whether real testimony is truthful.
- Agent A owns only `content/fiction-writing/` and `docs/planning/agent-a-fiction-writing/`. No shared TTS reader or other agents' material was edited.
