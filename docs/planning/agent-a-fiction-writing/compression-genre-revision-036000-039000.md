# Agent A — Compression, Genre, and Revision (036000–039000)

## Delivered manuscripts

- **036000, Short-Story Compression:** Poe's 1842 argument for unity of effect, Munro's life-spanning stories and revisions, and Lydia Davis's very short work. Uses a blue chair outside a closed laundromat to distinguish meaningful omission from mere withholding and arbitrary vagueness.
- **037000, Genre Hybridity:** Slipstream as a contested critical term, the editorial history of New Weird, and Kelly Link's mixed modes. The invented Office of Lost Hours tests causal, emotional, and epistemic reader promises.
- **038000, Revision as Re-Seeing:** Saunders on reader attention, McPhee on structure and revision in nonfiction, and Salesses on workshop norms. Multiple revisions of an invented greenhouse lock problem distinguish the scope and limitations of each tool.
- **039000, Comparative Workshop:** Four alternative treatments of a repaired radio whose voice seems to ask not to be returned. A practical synthesis in which different poetics generate incompatible drafts and require a conscious choice.
