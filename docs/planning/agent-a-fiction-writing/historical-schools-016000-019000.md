# Agent A — historical schools and institutional context (016000–019000)

**Status:** Four new substantive manuscripts, each independently listenable.

This batch follows `007500` and `008000–015000` without renumbering.

- **016000 — Modernism:** narrative consciousness and experienced time; differentiates free indirect discourse, interior monologue, and stream of consciousness. Uses a station-platform scene.
- **017000 — Latin American Boom:** formal experiments and the distinct commercial, geopolitical, and translation infrastructure of the Boom. Separates magical realism from the Boom; includes exclusion and precursors. Uses a disputed bridge photograph.
- **018000 — Postcolonial language and authority:** narrator location, re-writing, translated speech, Achebe–Ngũgĩ debate, multilingual worldbuilding. Uses a recovered school archive.
- **019000 — Institutions and genre:** pulps, paperbacks, MFA programs, author brands and digital serial publication. Uses a memory-rental manuscript pitched to several publishing venues.

Historical claims are bounded by track-local source records. Do not construe these as a complete history of world fiction. In particular, more tracks could cover African, Asian and Latin American literatures outside their international English-language reception, women and marginalized writers absent from canonical surveys, regional genre traditions, serial and fan communities, and reader-response/narratology.
