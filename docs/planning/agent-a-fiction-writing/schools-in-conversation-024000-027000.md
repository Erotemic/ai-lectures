## Editorial rationale

These lectures put recently introduced schools and individual writer positions in constructive tension rather than adding four more detached biographies. They are explicit thought experiments: the writers did not exchange these particular arguments in the historical record. A recurring bathhouse closure allows the comparison to be structural and ethical, not only terminological.

| Lecture | Comparison | Original demonstration | Editorial danger addressed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 024000 | Le Guin's carrier bag and goal-obstacle plot | Campaign outline, distributed ensemble, hybrid | Mistaking heroic plot for all causality; or a mosaic for a bag of unrelated scenes |
| 025000 | Gardner's moral dream and Barth's visible seams | Three incompatible reconstructions of one disputed document | Assuming immersive realism guarantees ethical seriousness, or formal artifice guarantees insight |
| 026000 | Beat-sheet architecture and Saunders's reader experience | Bad and revised openings; decision-map vs sentence map | Following beats while the reading is inert, or polishing scenes whose causal movement is circular |
| 027000 | Integrative craft judgment across schools | One brief, one hearing scene, multiple revisions | Turning schools into identities or universal prescriptions |
