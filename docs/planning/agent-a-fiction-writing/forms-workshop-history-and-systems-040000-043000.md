## References and epistemic boundaries

The four companion sections link to primary texts, official project documentation, and scholarly resources. Historical claim anchors are recorded in each sidecar. The fictional town, bridge, floodgate, bus station and ferry are original instructional constructs. Public dates and title details have been checked using publisher, author, scholarly or prize sources. Claim citations record references, not independent proof of every interpretive sentence.

Important distinctions: a short-story cycle is a critical classification, not a universal list of length rules; workshop procedures vary; Mantel's account is a practicing historical novelist's theory, while Hartman's essay addresses archives of slavery and should not be collapsed into a generic fiction method; Twine and Inform 7 expose different modes of interactive authoring.
