## Editorial boundaries

The material determines each lecture's length; no target/minimum/maximum duration policy applies. All vignettes and workshop exercises are invented and labeled as such. Bibliographies link to publisher, institution, DOI or scholarly records that were checked, but available abstracts are not presented as full-text evidence of a book's detailed results. Broad generalizations about national senses of humor, universal cross-cultural equivalence and automatic audience response are intentionally avoided.
