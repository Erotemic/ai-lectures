# Deep Time evidence map — first life, prebiotic chemistry, oxygen and LUCA

Research snapshot: 14 November 2026. Companion to lectures `014000`–`017000`. This file documents evidentiary roles rather than converting competing models into equal-probability hypotheses.

## Guardrails for the narration

- **Habitable ≠ inhabited.** Isotopic evidence for liquid water or reactive mineral surfaces does not establish organisms.
- **Candidate fossil ≠ uncontested fossil.** Evaluate primary structure, 3-D context, age relationship, metamorphism, contamination and realistic abiotic mimics.
- **Experiment ≠ event reconstruction.** Distinguish demonstrated laboratory mechanism, theoretical/computational model, geologically plausible environment, and direct evidence that it occurred.
- **Gross oxygen production ≠ atmospheric oxygen growth.** Atmospheric inventories depend on net production, chemical sinks, burial, geological recycling and climate feedbacks.
- **MIF threshold ≠ oxygen percentage.** The sedimentary transition restricts atmospheric photochemistry; oxygen magnitude depends on a model and is not uniformly measured over oceans.
- **LUCA ≠ first organism.** The most recent shared ancestry of extant sampled lineages says little directly about extinct earlier organisms or whether abiogenesis happened more than once.
- **Avoid manufacturing a unique timeline.** Geological fossils give minimum dates; molecular clocks give calibrated model estimates. Where intervals overlap or conflict, preserve the uncertainties.

## Reference hierarchy and live disagreements

1. **Biosignatures.** Noffke et al. (2013), Djokic et al. (2017), Nutman et al. (2016), Allwood et al. (2018), Dodd et al. (2017), and Bell–Fournier (2026). Present the Isua original and counterinterpretation together. The 2026 review explicitly separates ~3.5 Ga evidence from earlier candidates.
2. **Chemistry to cells.** Song et al. (2024) measured oligomers under cycling, Alejandre et al. (2025) modeled clay/water nucleic-acid replication, Abil et al. (2024) engineered a protocell with supplied enzyme systems. Short-chain-thioester membranes (2025) and abiotic synthetic vesicle reproduction (2025) concern compartments, not independent full biology. Higgs (2026) reviews RNA-world arguments and gaps.
3. **Oxygen.** The 2025 surface-ocean study and 2025 long-transition proxy series must be distinguished from 2026 reviews/modeled nutrient dynamics. Signatures are real but their global atmospheric translation can vary by orders of magnitude.
4. **LUCA.** Theobald (2010) supports universal ancestry under explicit models; Moody et al. (2024) makes specific ancestral genome and age estimates; 2015 transfer work and the 2026 Bell–Fournier review qualify how far reconstructions can be interpreted.
