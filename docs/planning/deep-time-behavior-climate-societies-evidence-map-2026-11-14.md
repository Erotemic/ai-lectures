# Deep Time: behaviors, climate, cultivation and societies (026000–029000)

Research snapshot: 14 November 2026. This map supplements `deep-time-evidence-reconstruction-track.md`. Each lecture has a standalone spoken manuscript, explicit evidence ledger, and source/claim records.

## Epistemic boundaries

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Burning ~1 Ma at Wonderwerk | Microscopic heated bone, ashed plants, contextual sediment | Fire occurred within an occupied cave | Natural fire versus deliberate ignition, local use versus widespread custom | More in-situ microstratigraphy and repeated layers, calibrated heat experiments |

| Younger Dryas climate change | Greenland chemical/isotopic annual-layer archives and regional proxies | Abrupt North Atlantic-centered climate shifts | Trigger strength, global synchrony; impact proposals contested | Independently dated, geographically broad proxies and ocean-circulation constraints |

## Lecture-by-lecture writing commitments

### 026000 — Fire, Food, Language, and the Evidence for Behavior

Separate thermal alteration from anthropogenic fire and fire maintenance from ignition. Explain how microscopy, heating experiments, fish-tooth analysis, and charred bread-like remains support particular behavioral reconstructions. For language, differentiate vocal anatomy, FOXP2, symbolic marks, grammar, and actual utterances; avoid asserting any unique prehistoric speech date.

### 027000 — Ice Ages and the Landscapes People Inhabited

Explain the different clocks in ice and trapped gas, isotope-proxy calibrations, Milankovitch seasonal forcing and internal climate feedbacks. Keep orbital cycles distinct from the still-disputed transition in glacial pacing. Explain the Younger Dryas as a strongly established climatic transition while treating its triggering mechanism and global consequences with regional specificity. Beyond EPICA has recovered ancient ice; complete analysis is ongoing.

### 028000 — Why Farming Began in Different Places

Define gathering, landscape management, cultivation and domestication separately. Distinguish observed charred cereal bases and phytoliths from a claim about a deliberate sowing decision. Compare southwest Asian cereals, lower Yangtze rice and Kuk Swamp wetland horticulture without representing one as a universal model. Discuss costs and benefits without claiming agriculture uniformly improved or harmed every population.

### 029000 — Before Writing: Networks, Rituals, and Political Organization

Do not equate monument size with statehood, grave wealth with lifetime political rank, obsidian source with a single trade caravan, or female-linked burials with proof of female political rule. Use Göbekli Tepe, Poverty Point and Çatalhöyük as distinct tests of social interpretation. Respect the limits of sampling, oral testimony, descendant communities and destructive DNA testing.

## Source provenance policy

The sidecars label cited claims `reference_recorded`, indicating a bibliographic link and a spoken anchor were checked. That field does **not** indicate that the entire peer-reviewed result has been independently replicated. Sources include primary research, institutional summaries and reviews; there are no invented quantitative confidence scores. Source dates are a 14 November 2026 research snapshot; new findings may change interpretations.
