# Gardening, Soil, and Landscaping: Living Systems and Water

Learn soil structure, plant physiology, drainage, amendments, ecological interactions and site diagnosis.

This is one of the 17 coordinated **practical-skills** tracks. Nineteen lectures (`001000`–`019000`) are authored. For the alternating rotation, follow the `020000` topic in `BACKLOG.md` and then visit the next track. These are educational drafts with non-spoken technical companions and provenance; no field certification or code-adoption check is implied.

Manuscripts have spoken-region markers, keeping the TTS narration separate from technical diagrams, notes, and references. Lecture length is determined by teaching needs, never a time target.
