# The World Reconsidered

Question-centered, standalone long-form audio lectures about evidence, reasoning,
individual and collective behavior, history, and the natural world. New track
inspired by `docs/planning/curriculum-expansion-world-reconsidered.md`.

Every lecture is independently comprehensible: a listener can start with any
recording. The historical and mathematical companions follow the spoken marker
and are excluded from audio synthesis. Sources, claim anchors and the lecture
manifest live in `metadata/` and `track.json`.

The subjects connect without requiring lecture-to-lecture references:

- **001000:** Counterfactuals, historical cholera investigations, Simpson's
  paradox, controlled experiments, potential outcomes.
- **002000:** Helmholtz, perceptual inference, Bartlett's reconstructive memory,
  eyewitness suggestion and flashbulb-memory experiments.
- **003000:** Kahneman and Tversky, natural-frequency reasoning, framing,
  conjunction errors, ecological rationality and forecast calibration.
- **004000:** Axelrod's cooperation tournaments, repeated games, the costs of
  monitoring, and Elinor Ostrom's research on governed commons.
- **005000:** Asch's conformity studies, Milgram's obedience studies and their
  reinterpretation, institutional abuses, engineering warnings and resistance.
- **006000:** Quetelet, Canguilhem, DSM history, distribution versus norms and accessible design.
- **007000:** Jane Jacobs, Schelling, Maxwell, feedback, networks, thresholds and system design.
- **008000:** July 1914, Tambora, counterfactual evidence, path dependence and hindsight bias.
- **009000:** Claude Shannon, entropy, prefix coding, noisy channels and semantic limitations.
- **010000:** Haber–Bosch fertilizer, Jevons rebound, CFCs, ozone monitoring and policy correction.
- **011000:** Epicurus, Aristotle, Epictetus, adult development, capabilities and well-being.
- **012000:** Szilard's petition, Asilomar, future people, uncertainty and discounting.

- **Audience:** general; any arithmetic, probability or game theory introduced
  in the recording, with a non-spoken mathematical companion as necessary.
- **Audio reader:** `/audio?track=world-reconsidered`
- **Lecture IDs:** six digits, unique within the track; do not renumber after publication.
- **Historical sources:** `metadata/sources.json`, supplemented by the
  per-lecture `metadata/<id>.json` claim-and-source sidecars. The status
  `reference_recorded` means a source reference and narration anchor were
  entered; it is not a certification that every wording was independently
  checked against the primary document.

All twelve flagship lectures now have substantive drafts. New companion tracks and proposed follow-on subjects remain tentative until their evidence and examples are developed.
