# The Physical World — Coverage and closure audit

This audit marks **what the sixteen-lecture introductory sequence teaches**, and what would need a separate course. It is an editorial assessment, not an automated duration or prose-quality test.

## Scope covered

| Domain | Relevant lectures | Strength and intended boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Measurement, force, conservation | 001000, 002000, 016000 | Elementary experiments, idealization, energy and prediction; not a full mechanics calculus sequence |
| Heat, entropy, probability | 002000, 003000, 011000, 015000 | Equilibrium, heat engines, microscopic statistics, phase changes; no full statistical-mechanics derivation |
| Fields, circuits, light | 004000, 005000, 008000, 009000 | Local field models, electromagnetism, signal propagation, optical observation; not field-theory problem sets |
| Fluids and weather | 007000, 016000 | Conservation equations and turbulence, with the limits of forecasting; no full nonlinear-fluid mechanics course |
| Atomic and material properties | 006000, 010000, 015000 | Quantum energy scales, bonding, crystals, nucleation, collective order; chemistry and condensed matter remain separate specializations |
| Spacetime and gravity | 012000, 016000 | Proper time, simultaneity, general relativity at a conceptual level, clock evidence; not differential geometry |
| Quantum physics | 013000, 016000 | Coherent amplitudes, measurement, uncertainty, entanglement, experiments; not a mathematical quantum-mechanics curriculum |
| Dynamical systems | 005000, 014000, 016000 | Oscillation, resonance, coupled modes and feedback, plus chaos as a predictability boundary; not control theory in depth |
| Scientific synthesis | 016000 | Connecting scales, the role of effective descriptions, what experiments establish, and open limits |

## Unresolved matters properly retained as limits

- Physics still has unanswered questions about dark matter, dark energy, quantum gravity and interpretations of quantum measurement. These do not invalidate the experimentally supported regimes explained in the track.
- Several lecture topics are developed conceptually and through spoken numerical examples rather than full mathematical proofs. That reflects the track's general-audience remit, not a claim to teach specialist mathematics.
- Microscopic laws alone do not make large-scale models unnecessary; phenomenological parameters and collective variables are central to explaining fluids, materials, and biological systems.
- The complement to *The Living World* is a **separate cell-biology and genetics course**, not another appendix to this physical-world series.

## Editorial status

**Complete.** Lecture 016000 is the final lecture in the main route. New independent specialist tracks can be developed without reopening this inventory.
