### Editorial boundaries

- The inverse and implicit function theorems are **local**; rank loss does not determine the geometry of every singular level set.
- Poincare lemma concerns positive-degree closed forms on star-shaped neighborhoods; de Rham's theorem is a further global result, not a synonym.
- The cycle integral pairing is defined first; its being an isomorphism is a separate theorem.
- Flat connections can have nontrivial holonomy; trivial bundles can have curved connections.
- The unitary convention for the final manuscript is `nabla=d+A`, `F=dA+A wedge A`, and `c1=[i F/(2 pi)]`. The sphere example has Chern number `+1` with the stated orientation.
