# Number Theory from First Principles

A proof-driven course connecting integer arithmetic with algorithms, algebraic structure, and analytic number theory. It starts with gcds, congruences, integer equations, reciprocity, and finite fields, and develops the analytic machinery for complete proofs of **Dirichlet's theorem**, the **prime number theorem**, and **Dedekind prime-ideal factorization**, with ideal norms, and now connects splitting of primes to quadratic forms and ideal classes. The course currently contains **48 substantive lectures**.

The opening lectures assume only integer arithmetic, fractions, elementary algebra, and a willingness to follow proofs. Later lectures build their own analysis and character foundations rather than presupposing advanced number theory. Each manuscript has one extractable spoken lecture and a display-only mathematical companion with worked equations, exercises, claim-level reading records, and Wikipedia references. Length and word counts describe the manuscripts; they are not duration targets.

## Proven theorems and open boundaries

- **001000–006000:** Euclidean algorithm and prime factorization; CRT, order and Euler's theorem; Diophantine equations and Pythagorean triples; continued fractions; quadratic residues.
- **007000–010000:** quadratic reciprocity proved by Gauss's lemma, Dirichlet convolution and Möbius inversion, elementary prime-distribution arguments, and simple-root Hensel lifting.
- **011000–014000:** Pell equation existence and classification for positive norm-one solutions, Gaussian integers and the two-squares criterion, primality witnesses/certificates, and finite-field multiplicative groups including primitive roots.
- **015000–018000:** series and summation by parts, character construction and orthogonality, Euler products and von Mangoldt weights, and the character projection argument for primes in progressions. Lecture 018000 deliberately identifies the then-unproved general nonvanishing theorem; the gap is filled by the new sequence below.
- **019000–023000, Continuation 5:** partial-summation continuation of zeta and nonprincipal L-series to the right half-plane; full nonvanishing for nonreal characters via a trigonometric square; full nonvanishing and positivity for real characters via an explicitly proved Landau disk lemma; **unconditional proof of Dirichlet's theorem for every coprime residue and modulus**, including divergent prime reciprocals and equal Dirichlet densities; and elementary \(\psi(x)=\Theta(x)\), \(\pi(x)=\Theta(x/\log x)\) estimates using binomial coefficients.

- **024000–025500, Continuation 6:** exact signed, ordered representation count for sums of two squares and its \(r_2/4=\mathbf1*\chi_4\) formula; elementary Gauss circle lattice-point estimate \(N(R)=\pi R^2+O(R)\); existence and uniqueness of finite fields of every prime-power order; Frobenius orbits, subfield structure, exact irreducible counts, Rabin irreducibility test, and trace/norm. The refined Gauss circle error problem and the prime number theorem remain outside this proof sequence.

- **026000–026500, Continuation 7 (analytic branch):** triangle-level Cauchy-Goursat proof, circle integral formula, Taylor/Laurent and residue toolkit, prime-power logarithmic derivative, and a complete zero-free proof for the zeta function on \(\Re(s)=1\). The Tauberian implication \(\psi(x)\sim x\) remains a stated theorem to be proved or carefully applied later.
- **027000–027500, Continuation 7 (algebraic branch):** exact quadratic ring-of-integers classification and discriminants, the failure of element unique factorization in \(\mathbb Z[\sqrt{-5}]\), explicit prime-ideal products, a self-contained two-dimensional Minkowski argument, and class group \(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z\) for \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-5})\). The **general** Dedekind ideal-factorization/invertibility theorem is clearly cited but not fully proved here.

**Completed in Continuation 8:**

- **026750:** Newman's bounded-function Tauberian theorem, with the two-contour residue identity, exact endpoint cancellation, and correctly ordered limits.
- **027625:** general number-field integral lattice via the trace pairing; proof that every ring of integers is Noetherian, integrally closed and one-dimensional.
- **027750:** the prime-product lemma, invertibility of every nonzero prime ideal, and existence and uniqueness of prime-ideal factorization for all nonzero integral and fractional ideals.
- **027875:** local Dedekind rings are DVRs, prime-power residue layers have size \(N(\mathfrak p)\), and \(N(IJ)=N(I)N(J)\), completing the cited algebraic prerequisites of the earlier class-group calculation.
- **028000:** a complete proof that \(\psi(x)\sim x\), \(\vartheta(x)\sim x\) and \(\pi(x)\sim x/\log x\). The Tauberian and unsmoothing steps are proved rather than cited.

**Not claimed as proved:** quantitative zero-free regions or error terms for prime counting, the prime number theorem in arbitrary arithmetic progressions, the Riemann hypothesis, the refined Gauss circle error problem, or the p-adic local-global theorems. The earlier historical descriptions of missing proofs in Continuation 7 remain accurate for *those installments*; the newly supplied lectures close the two identified proof gaps.


**Completed in Continuation 9:**

- **029000:** quadratic splitting, inertia, and ramification from the integral minimal polynomial, with a proof for each case and an independent treatment of the prime 2.
- **029250:** Dedekind-Kummer factorization with the prime-to-index hypothesis, the residue degree identity \(\sum_i e_i f_i=[K:\mathbb Q]\), and the counterexample to using \(\sqrt5\) modulo 2.
- **029500:** the oriented ideal-lattice construction of primitive positive definite forms of fundamental negative discriminant, including inverse construction and proper-equivalence convention.
- **029750:** a terminating reduction algorithm, bound on reduced leading coefficients, and explicit class groups at discriminants \(-20\) and \(-23\).
- **030000:** prime representation by ideal norm forms, and a proof of the exact mod-20 classifications for the two forms of discriminant \(-20\).

**At the end of Continuation 9:** the general ramification-discriminant theorem via the different, nonmaximal quadratic orders and ring class groups, indefinite form reduction and narrow class groups, higher-degree splitting algorithms, and local fields remained to be developed.

**Completed in Continuation 10 (local foundations):**

- **030250:** p-adic valuations, the ultrametric triangle inequality, clopen balls, and rational geometric-series convergence.
- **030500:** Cauchy-sequence construction of Q_p, nonzero inverses, completeness, and unique p-adic digits.
- **030750:** Z_p as the inverse limit of residue rings, its units and ideals, compactness, and the composite-base CRT distinction.
- **031000:** complete simple-root Hensel proof and strengthened Newton lifting, with p=2 and singular-root examples.
- **031250:** full local square criteria for odd p and p=2, the local obstruction to x²+y²=3, and a direct local-global result for rational squares. Hasse–Minkowski and Selmer's counterexample are cited, not proved.
- **031500:** explicit complete quadratic extensions Q_3(√2) and Q_3(√3), with their valuations, residue fields, ramification indexes, and a split Q_3×Q_3 example. The general finite-extension theorem is cited, not proved.

**Remaining boundaries:** full generality of finite extensions of complete DVRs; the Hasse–Minkowski proof; local Hilbert symbols; different/discriminant and ramification; nonmaximal quadratic orders, narrow classes, and advanced analytic number theory.
