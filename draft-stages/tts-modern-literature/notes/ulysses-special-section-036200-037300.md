# Ulysses: editorial dossier and source trail

## Why this work now

This is a high-impact landmark selected for formal and historical influence, not as a representation quota. The novel has disproportionate value for teaching narrative consciousness, an episodic architecture that changes style, the transformation of epic into ordinary social life, and the legal history of literary expression. One full work is treated as an extended section, not split into equal-duration episodes.

## Documentary base and limits

**Primary novel:** James Joyce, *Ulysses*, Project Gutenberg ebook 4300, derived from pre-1923 editions. The U.S. ebook is public domain. Plot statements are tied to the original novel rather than film, musical, or one secondary summary. This is an original prose explanation, not a substitute copy of Joyce's text. The primary supports the events and internal literary form; biographical and publication dates are corroborated separately.

**Scholarly / documentary cross-checks:** Morgan Library's centenary exhibition (intro, Homeric schema, changing drafts, publication, Molly and Nora, Nausicaa censorship); Pericles Lewis's Yale Modernism Lab account of the move beyond initial interior monologue; Yale Modernism Lab's study of Nighttown; University at Buffalo's Sylvia Beach catalogue for the 1933–34 American publishing sequence. Source catalog entries mark which resources are full online pages, preview-only scholarship, or published primary works.

**Explicit distinctions:**

- There are 18 episodes, but Homeric episode names are traditionally used editorial/critical designations; avoid saying the first edition visibly printed them as its own headings.
- Joyce's schemes are documented authorial materials, but their system was developed and circulated selectively; it does not mechanically explain or determine every interpretation.
- Stephen's Shakespeare hypothesis is deliberately contested and should not be recast as archival proof about William Shakespeare.
- `Cyclops` employs a hostile, unnamed narrator and self-satirizing hypertrophic rhetoric. Anti-Jewish statements by the Citizen and Deasy are false prejudice, not editorial claims. Bloom's religious and national identity is not reducible to one label.
- In `Nausicaa`, Gerty and Bloom's public sexual interaction is explicit; consent and narrative perspective require interpretive care. The fact that Bloom later observes a limp must not be turned into a disability-based moral verdict.
- In `Circe`, bizarre transformations, accusations, spirits and Rudy's appearance are staged imaginatively, not real events testified to by Dublin's entire population. The physical assault of Stephen, broken lamp and Bloom's aid belong to the outward action.
- `Penelope` is a fictional monologue with strong stylistic links to Nora Barnacle letters, not a first-person record of Nora. Molly's closing yes is a remembered affirmation, not proof that the marital breach is resolved.
- The Little Review serial publication, the 1921 obscenity prosecution of Anderson and Heap, the Paris publication by Sylvia Beach in 1922, and the US Woolsey ruling in December 1933 (authorized US edition 1934) are separate stages. Do not say a single global ban or repeal applied to all countries.
- Joyce revised extensively in proofs and later editions vary. The 1984 Gabler edition became important and disputed; we do not claim the original text is textually unproblematic.

## Suggested editorial follow-ups

The complete story and seven analyses should be read as a coherent series. Optional future enrichment would use short *primary-text close-readings with exact episode/line references by a declared edition*, expanded coverage of the music in Sirens, or a comparative lecture on Joyce, Proust, and Woolf with independently grounded publication histories. Such improvements should follow source review; this package is not a claim that every allusion has been annotated.

## Verification checklist

The files in this pack carry spoken region markers and metadata sidecars. `track.json` lists exactly those manuscripts, and word counts are mechanically measured rather than length requirements. Every `historical_claims.anchor` occurs exactly once in its transcript's extracted spoken text and every source ID resolves in `metadata/sources.json`. No binary assets, UI changes, or shared code are part of the change.
