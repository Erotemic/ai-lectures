# Dante listening guide (non-spoken)

The Dante section runs **016000–037000** in the existing *Literary Classics
Before 1900* track. It is written for listeners unfamiliar with the poem and
includes complete spoilers for the three cantiche.

## Narrative journey — 12 lectures

| Realm | Episodes | Canto ranges |
|---|---|---|
| **Inferno** | 016000–019000 | I–V; VI–XVII; XVIII–XXX; XXXI–XXXIV |
| **Purgatorio** | 020000–023000 | I–IX; X–XVII; XVIII–XXVII; XXVIII–XXXIII |
| **Paradiso** | 024000–027000 | I–IX; X–XVII; XVIII–XXVII; XXVIII–XXXIII |

These twelve lectures cover the story's **100 cantos** in order. They prioritize
important encounters, narrative decisions, and meaningful changes in the way
Dante sees; they are not canto-by-canto recitations or claim to include every
person or conversation. Each can be heard without reading the Italian first.

## Critical lectures — 10 lectures

- 028000: fictional pilgrim versus later writer; time and design.
- 029000: judgment, contrapasso, sympathy and its difficulties.
- 030000: free will, penitence, habit and community on the mountain.
- 031000: Paradise as a poetry of thinking and knowledge.
- 032000: Florence, exile, papal power and imperial ideals.
- 033000: guides, women's voices, Beatrice and Virgil.
- 034000: Italian, terza rima, sound and translation.
- 035000: medieval cosmology, philosophical and theological assumptions.
- 036000: worked close readings across three realms.
- 037000: manuscripts, editing, translations, reception and influence.

A useful listening pattern is all twelve story lectures followed by whichever
critical strands answer the listener's questions. Reading the whole poem is
not an assumed prerequisite. No duration or word-count target determined the
section structure.
