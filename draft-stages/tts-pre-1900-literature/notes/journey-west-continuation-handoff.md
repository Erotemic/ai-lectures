## Where the original writer continues

**Continue from the opening of selected-witness chapter 88**, after lecture `560000`. The travelers have left **Fengxian Prefecture**, where Wukong obtained rain only after learning why Heaven had prohibited it. The prefect publicly repented, the district prayed and did works of compassion, divine officers delivered rain, and a Sweet Rain/Universal Relief temple was raised. The pilgrims accepted farewell hospitality and resumed the western road. **The three-year drought is resolved.** Do not carry it into Jade Flower Kingdom or skip the new kingdom's opening scene.

The six most recent chapters preserve these transitions and endpoints:

- **Chapter 82 (`555000`)**: Bajie descends to scout Trap-Sky Mountain's Bottomless Cave after chapter 81's abduction. He reports a vast underground dwelling. Wukong, as an insect, finds Xuanzang and discovers the rat spirit's coercive wedding feast. The first wine-froth trick fails when its bubbles break; Wukong returns transformed as a peach and enters the captor's belly. **This chapter ends with the struggle and attempted escape unresolved**, not with Li Jing's intervention.
- **Chapter 83 (`556000`)**: The white rat's second embroidered-shoe substitution carries Xuanzang away. Wukong discovers cave tablets honoring **Pagoda-Bearing Heavenly King Li Jing and Nezha**, then brings a complaint before Heaven. Nezha explains that the rat was spared and recognized as an adopted daughter; his father and heavenly troops descend. The spirit is caught and **Xuanzang rescued** by the chapter's end. The novel's attribution of responsibility and divine favor is part of the plot, not a simple endorsement of heavenly bureaucracy.
- **Chapter 84 (`557000`)**: At **Dharma-Destroying Kingdom**, the ruler vows to kill **10,000 monks** and has already killed **9,996**, with four left to complete the tally. Guanyin warns the pilgrims; Wukong arranges merchant disguises and an innkeeper's chest. Robbers steal the chest and royal troops recover it. Wukong magically shaves the king, officials, and entire sleeping court. **The ruler's reform is not in this chapter; it opens chapter 85.**
- **Chapter 85 (`558000`)**: The shaven king repents and the kingdom is renamed **Dharma-Honoring Kingdom**. In the mountains the disciples face a demon who deploys false troop formations as distractions; Xuanzang is carried off to the leopard demon's Hidden Mist Mountain cave, where a woodcutter is also captive. The master is still lost at the chapter's end.
- **Chapter 86 (`559000`)**: The captor displays **false severed heads**, including a staged image of Xuanzang's death, while the disciples struggle to distinguish deceit from fact. Wukong infiltrates and finds Xuanzang and the woodcutter alive. The leopard is defeated, the mountain dwelling burned, and the woodcutter returns to his aged mother. This rescue is resolved before chapter 87.
- **Chapter 87 (`560000`)**: **Fengxian Prefecture** has suffered three years of drought. A magistrate insulted an offering in anger; Heaven imposed three impossible-seeming restrictions on rain: a rice mountain pecked by a chicken, a flour mountain licked by a dog, and a golden lock consumed by candle flame. The governor's repentance and the people's good deeds fulfill these in the novel's moral cosmology. Wukong and celestial rain officers bring a downpour. Temple dedication and departure **end chapter 87**.
