# Comparative physiology: further lectures

The first ten manuscripts are complete and indexed, including the three sensory chapters (`008000`–`010000`). Later topics should remain coherent, substantive spoken lectures with specific source-backed metadata, not a predetermined number or runtime target. Build chapters when the science supports a complete argument and useful comparisons.

## Completed this iteration

- **008000 — How a Body Knows Where It Is:** skin and limb mechanoreception, proprioception, insect strain sensors, fish lateral line, balance and hearing.
- **009000 — What Light Becomes:** optics, rods and cones, retinal signaling, low-light uncertainty and behavioral color-vision experiments.
- **010000 — Senses Without a Picture:** olfactory coding, interrupted odor plumes, contact taste, electric senses and thermal pit organs.
