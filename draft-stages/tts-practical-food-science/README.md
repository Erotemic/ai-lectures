# Cooking and Food Science: Heat, Water, Structure, and Flavor

Understand cooking as control of heat transfer, protein and starch changes, water, texture, flavor and hygiene.

This is one of the 17 coordinated **practical-skills** tracks. Twenty lectures (`001000`–`020000`) are authored. For the alternating rotation, follow the `021000` topic in `BACKLOG.md` and then visit the next track. These are educational drafts with non-spoken technical companions and provenance; no field certification or code-adoption check is implied.

Manuscripts have spoken-region markers, keeping the TTS narration separate from technical diagrams, notes, and references. Lecture length is determined by teaching needs, never a time target.
