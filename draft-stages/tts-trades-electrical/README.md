# Electrical Systems: Principles, Practice, Codes, and Judgment

**Trades Foundations — electrical track.** For a homeowner or beginner seeking electrical vocabulary, a rigorous account of safety, and the ability to plan and critically evaluate repair and upgrade work. Assumed background: ordinary arithmetic and willingness to reason about cause and effect. No prior electrical work required.

This course is not authorization to work on hazardous circuits, a substitute for field qualification or a substitute for adopted law, inspection, or manufacturer documentation. Professional and supervised work remain central to some competencies; those boundaries are explicit rather than disguised.

**Teaching policy:** Three layers in every subject: enduring physical hazards; engineering methods that manage those hazards; locally enforceable codes, permissions and inspection. U.S. residential examples are common, but the course distinguishes them from universal laws and compares international practice. No edition-specific code numbers are assumed evergreen.

**Length and editorial policy:** The material determines lecture length. There is no ten-minute target, fixed duration range, minimum/maximum word count, or mandated section count. Counts in `track.json` describe completed narration only; neither counts nor listening time are quality gates. Prefer the explanation, examples, and safety distinctions the subject genuinely requires.

## Exercises

Current published lessons use desk calculations, visual-only surveys and controlled low-voltage educational simulations. They do not require opening an electrical panel, exposing wiring or making live measurements. See each lecture's companion section for the concrete activity and its boundary.
