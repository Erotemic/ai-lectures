# Mathematical Reasoning and Proof from First Principles

An introductory course in writing and reading rigorous mathematical arguments. Builds from statements, quantifiers, definitions, and proof methods to sets, functions, equivalence relations, induction, infinity, existence/uniqueness, epsilon proofs, and compactness. Designed as a bridge to Analysis, Abstract Algebra, Topology, Discrete Mathematics, and Logic/Type Theory.

The current pack contains **3 lecture manuscripts**. Short concept chapters have been consolidated where they develop one subject. Each numbered manuscript has one spoken region; its mathematical statements, exercises, sources, and inherited component notes remain in the non-spoken companion.
