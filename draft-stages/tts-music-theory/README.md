# Music Theory and Composition

**Opening collection:** Four substantive, standalone, audio-first lecture manuscripts. Every musical example is original and reproducible by voice, tapping, or ordinary instruments; no embedded or generated audio is required.

## Completed lectures

- **001000** — [Music as Pattern in Time: A Listener's Way into Composition](001000-music-as-pattern-in-time.md). 2,214 spoken words, approximately 15.3 minutes at 145 words per minute.
- **002000** — [Pitch, Intervals, and the Shape of a Melody](002000-pitch-intervals-and-melodic-shape.md). 2,554 spoken words, approximately 17.6 minutes at 145 words per minute.
- **003000** — [Rhythm, Meter, and Groove: How Music Organizes Time](003000-rhythm-meter-and-groove.md). 2,674 spoken words, approximately 18.4 minutes at 145 words per minute.
- **004000** — [Scales, Chords, and Harmonic Direction: Compose a Four-Bar Phrase](004000-scales-chords-and-composing-a-phrase.md). 2,895 spoken words, approximately 20.0 minutes at 145 words per minute.

## Course design

The sequence begins with listening and original sound gestures, introduces a specific named pitch system without equating it to all music, builds metric and rhythmic tools, then integrates melody and harmony in a fully specified miniature. Lectures are standalone for out-of-order listening but cumulative as composition exercises. Every lecture has a spoken region, non-spoken workbook, focused source links, and an individual metadata sidecar.
