# Water Margin: a long-form narrative and evidence sequence

This series follows *Water Margin* (*Shuihu zhuan*), drawing the principal narrative line from the 100-chapter and 120-chapter traditions and explicitly discussing Jin Shengtan’s shorter, alternate ending. The work is traditionally associated with Shi Nai’an (and sometimes Luo Guanzhong), while its authorship and formation remain debated. The Northern Song setting, historical references to Song Jiang, earlier story traditions, and surviving printed recensions are distinguished in the spoken lectures rather than pooled as a single source of fact.

## Continuing narrative — planned, no stubs included

A later volume should tell the remaining story in full, rather than treating it as a short epilogue. Suggested arcs: further battles and the government assaults; Chao Gai’s death and Lu Junyi; completion of the 108 and their celestial rankings; rival arguments over amnesty and the defeat of imperial armies; the Liao campaign; the additional Tian Hu and Wang Qing campaigns specifically identified as 120-chapter material; Fang La and the losses of the brotherhood; poisoned wine, final departures, and the divergent conclusions of the recensions. The final historical analysis should compare the historical Fang La uprising with the novel’s war narrative. No later lectures are marked complete in this volume.

## Editorial practice

- Use a complete spoken narrative, not a plot synopsis or a series of character facts. Each recording supplies the context required to follow it on its own.
- Distinguish the historical Northern Song and records of a band under Song Jiang from the literary scenes and biographies; never claim an individual fictional confrontation is confirmed by those records.
- Treat official corruption and the brothers’ own violence with the same specificity. In particular, retain the agency and coercive circumstances of Pan Jinlian, Pan Qiaoyun, Hu Sanniang, and other women without presenting revenge as neutral justice.
- Keep citations, sources, manuscript variants and chapter-count details in the research apparatus when they would interrupt the story; introduce the material evidence in the spoken narration when it changes interpretation.
- Add future lectures using `tools/merge_world_history_overlay.py --zip`, never direct `unzip -o` into the repository. In a concurrent checkout, inspect collision reports and allocate another unused numeric range rather than overwriting existing work.
