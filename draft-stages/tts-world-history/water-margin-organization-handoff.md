navigation prerequisite. Do not create a placeholder recording, substitute a
first installment for an unqualified full-story overview, or label the source

## Version decisions and source checks

- **70-chapter Jin Shengtan form:** inspected chapter 70 stages the assembly
  and then Lu Junyi's nightmare. A bow-bearing figure subdues him; the other
  107 arrive bound, and all 108 are executed inside the dream. This is neither
  the long amnesty/campaign plot nor an uncomplicated ending of eternal happy
  fellowship. Numbering may count the prologue separately. Do not describe the
  dream execution as an actual event in the waking plot, and do not assume
  every reader's 70/71 label denotes identical chapter accounting.
- **100-chapter form:** inspected contents continue through amnesty, Liao,
  Fang La, and the tragic conclusion. It does not contain the extra Tian Hu
  and Wang Qing campaigns supplied by the 120-chapter form.
- **120-chapter form:** inspected final chapter distinguishes Lu Junyi's
  poisoning and subsequent drowning from Song Jiang's poisoned imperial wine.
  Song Jiang then poisons Li Kui himself, fearing the latter's renewed
  rebellion would destroy their loyal reputation. Wu Yong and Hua Rong's
  deaths by hanging at the grave are separate again. Do not collapse these
  into one mass poisoning or assign Li Kui's poisoning directly to the court.
- Keep fictional individual adventures and speeches distinct from evidence
  for the actual Northern Song or a historical Song Jiang/Fang La. Traditional
  attribution to Shi Nai'an, with Luo Guanzhong in some accounts, is contested.
