## Highest-priority remaining foundational gaps

1. **Numerical time stepping.** Build from Euler to midpoint and Runge–Kutta, local versus global truncation error, and a concrete stiff decay test with step-size stability.
2. **Elementary boundary-value problems and Fourier modes.** Distinguish boundary from initial conditions and show eigenfunctions of the one-dimensional Laplacian before advanced heat and wave equations.
3. **Fourier transforms versus Fourier series.** Explain what changes when signals do not repeat, how a continuum of frequencies appears, and when transform methods are useful.
