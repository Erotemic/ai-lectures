## Highest-priority remaining foundational gaps

1. **Conservation-law and transport discretization from first principles.** Build upwind differences using one-sided transport, show why a centered advection scheme can oscillate, and derive the Courant number and conservation check with a tiny grid.
2. **Discrete Fourier transform and sampling.** Relate the continuous transform to actual sampled data, aliasing, the Nyquist frequency, and the fast Fourier transform without assuming signal-processing experience.
3. **Practical integration error controls.** Develop absolute and relative scales for vector states, rejection near switched inputs, and a simple energy-preserving oscillator integrator.
