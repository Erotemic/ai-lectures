# History · People and ideas

The playable History track contains **ten researched long-form biographies**:
Camille Jordan, Lord Rayleigh, Walther Ritz, Hermann Weyl, Richard Courant,
Ernst Fischer, David Hilbert, Tosio Kato, Chandler Davis, and William Kahan.

The 5,836-word Fischer lecture develops the 1905 min–max result, Riesz–Fischer
completeness and coefficient realization, the 1908 positive-block determinant
inequality, Fischer’s documented influence on Emmy Noether, and his forced 1938
retirement under Nazi racial policy. Mathematical details and source distinctions
remain in the non-spoken companion.

Estimated listening lengths remain about **27–41 minutes** at 145 spoken words
per minute. Actual TTS length depends on voice, speed and pauses. Audio is
not regenerated by editorial changes.

The remaining **18 short drafts** are under `stubs/` with their metadata and
original IDs. They are excluded from the playable catalog until substantially
researched and expanded. The reader isolates progress, audio caches and
feedback for this track; lectures need not be heard in sequence.
