# History · People and ideas

The playable History track contains **nine researched long-form biographies**:
Camille Jordan, Lord Rayleigh, Walther Ritz, Hermann Weyl, Richard Courant,
David Hilbert, Tosio Kato, Chandler Davis, and William Kahan.

Courant’s 4,612-word lecture develops the Dirichlet principle, the min–max
characterization of eigenvalues, nodal domains, the CFL condition and
triangle-based variational approximation, with a substantive career history.
Mathematical details and provenance remain in the non-spoken companion.

Estimated listening lengths remain about **27–35 minutes** at 145 spoken words
per minute. Actual TTS length depends on voice, speed and pauses. Audio is
not regenerated by editorial changes.

The remaining **19 short drafts** are under `stubs/` with their metadata and
original IDs. They are excluded from the playable catalog until substantially
researched and expanded. The reader isolates progress, audio caches and
feedback for this track; lectures need not be heard in sequence.
