# History · People and ideas

The playable History track contains **21 researched long-form biographies**, from
Camille Jordan through Gene H. Golub, covering geometry, variational analysis,
operator theory, and numerical computation. Every lecture is a standalone audio
narrative with mathematical prerequisites stated in its heading.

**Gene H. Golub** (`022000`) is a **6,101-word** narrated historical portrait
(approximately **42.1 minutes** at 145 WPM). It covers his Chicago
upbringing, Illinois training and ILLIAC work, Chebyshev iterative methods,
Cambridge and the collaboration with William Kahan, the 1965 bidiagonal SVD
algorithm and 1970 work with Christian Reinsch, reliable least squares,
principal angles with Åke Björck, total least squares with Charles Van Loan,
conjugate gradients and Poisson solvers, and his teaching, mentorship,
and organizing work at Stanford and SIAM. Worked examples explain rank loss,
condition-number squaring, pseudoinverses, orthogonal fitting, and subspace angles.
The display-only companion contains formulas and primary-source references.

Szőkefalvi-Nagy (`013000`), and Ky Fan (`012000`).
