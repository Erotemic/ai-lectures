# History · People and ideas

The playable History track contains **22 researched long-form biographies**, from
Camille Jordan through G. W. Stewart, covering geometry, variational analysis,
operator theory, and numerical computation. Each recording is an independent
historical narrative with its mathematical background declared in the heading.

**G. W. “Pete” Stewart** (`023000`) is a **5,909-word** historical portrait
(approximately **40.8 minutes** at 145 words per minute), covering his
Knoxville upbringing, Oak Ridge programming, doctorate with Alston Householder,
and work at Texas, Carnegie Mellon and the University of Maryland; the
Bartels–Stewart Sylvester solver, Moler–Stewart QZ algorithm, 1973 invariant
subspace perturbation analysis, LINPACK, QR updating, stochastic perturbations,
and the Stewart–Sun book. It also discusses his 1995 Gauss translation and
2026 death, and contains worked examples of subspace graph correction,
non-normal eigenvalue sensitivity, Sylvester separation, and least-squares
conditioning. The display-only companion contains complete derivations,
chronology and source citations.

Previously expanded portraits include Golub (`022000`), Björck (`021000`),
Wedin (`020000`), Rosenblum (`017000`), Dixmier (`015000`), Halmos (`014000`),
Szőkefalvi-Nagy (`013000`), and Ky Fan (`012000`).

The remaining **6 shorter sketches** are retained in `stubs/` and excluded
